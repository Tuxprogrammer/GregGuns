# Pack integration notes

GregGuns is built on GregTech, so most of what you already know applies.

- **GregTech tiers and materials.** Guns, ammo and armor are made from GregTech materials and parts, in GregTech machines, and unlock with GregTech tiers (Steam to ZPM). Nothing here has its own power system.
- **EU and charging.** Energy guns and powered armor store EU. Charge each one in a GregTech Charger or Battery Buffer of exactly its tier.
- **NEI.** The Ammo Press has its own recipe tab, and every GregGuns item is listed in build order.
- **Questbook.** GregGuns has its own tab in the GregTech New Horizons questbook, after Kill All the Things. Early quests pay ammo or a magazine for the gun they teach, and chapter ends and boss hunts pay a GregTech loot bag of their tier. A reward never outruns its tier.
- **Infernal Mobs.** Infernal GregGuns enemies drop tiered ammo, magazines and a gun of their own tier.
- **Teams.** On a server with ServerUtilities, your team's members and allies count as friends for turret and Rampart security.
- **Equipment slots.** Back gear, masks and the ammo pouch use the extra slots the pack already provides.
- **Guide.** Hold **G** over a GregGuns item for its page, or press **G** and pick GregGuns.
