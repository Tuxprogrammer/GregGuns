# Enemies and loot

Who shoots back, where you will meet them, and what they carry and drop. Every faction can be switched off in the config. They do not fight each other, but they will shoot your turrets. Their guns hit softer than yours as the gun's tier climbs, and their aim wanders with distance.

## In the wild: the Overworld

The danger level rises the farther you travel from world spawn, and in harsh biomes: deserts, wastelands, frozen lands and swamps. Higher danger opens tougher spawns.

| Enemy | Danger | Carries | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zombie Farmer | 0+ | A hoe or a Hand Cannon, in Miner's clothes. | Wheat, seeds, leather, bread, apples, Tanned Leather, and 2 to 6 Stone Bullets. |
| Zombie Miner | 0+ | A pickaxe or a Hand Cannon, and a Miner's Helmet. | Coal, redstone, iron, gunpowder, Tanned Leather, and 2 to 6 Stone Bullets. |
| Zombie Soldier | 1+ | A revolver, a Thompson or a shovel, in Soldier armor. | Shotgun shells, 9mm bullets, iron, gunpowder, Tanned Leather. |
| Skeleton Soldier | 1+ | A revolver, Thompson or Hand Cannon; a helmet and boots. | Bones, coal, gunpowder, iron, Tanned Leather, 9mm and Stone Bullets. |
| Psycho Steve | 1+, rare boss | A Chainsaw and red Miner armor. Very fast, very angry. | Always his Chainsaw and 4 to 8 full Fuel Tanks. |
| Bandit | 2+ | A pistol, revolver, Sawed Off, Thompson, AK47 or Bolt Action Rifle, in Bandit armor. | Ammo, iron, gunpowder, Tanned Leather. |

## In the Nether

Nether biomes only. The Nether's danger is the Overworld distance a spot leads to (eight blocks per Nether block), plus 1, so the tougher spawns sit farther from your portal.

| Enemy | Where | Carries | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zombie Pigman Soldier | Anywhere, alone or in pairs. The common one. | Combat armor and a Thompson, AK47, Flamethrower, LMG, Rocket Launcher or more. | Steel, gold, gunpowder, shotgun shells, rifle bullets, 9mm bullets, 1 or 2 rockets and a full Fuel Tank. |
| Super Mutants (Basic, Heavy, Elite) | Basic from about 60 Nether blocks out, Heavy from about 125, Elite from about 310. | Each rank is bigger. A Rocket Launcher, AK47, Combat Shotgun or Laser Rifle. | Cybernetic Parts, blaze rods, ghast tears, rockets, rifle bullets, shotgun shells. |
| Cyberdemon (boss) | Rare, deep in the Nether, always alone, never within 128 blocks of another. | Fireproof, with a Nether Blaster. | Cybernetic Parts, blaze rods, ghast tears. |

## In the End

Commando squads of two or three roam the End's outer islands. None spawn within 256 blocks of the End's center, so the dragon's island stays clear, and the farther out you go the better their gear.

## At camps, bases and nests

Military Camps, Military Bases and Aircraft Carriers fly a <ItemLink id="gregguns:camp_flag" /> that keeps calling in soldiers until you break it. Breaking it brings the helicopter. About one Military Base in five is a red base: it flies a red flag over a red pad, with red standards at its corners, and Dictator Dave holds the post by the flag and stays there. Blue bases and the Aircraft Carrier fly blue flags and have no commander.

| Enemy | Where | Carries | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Army Soldier | Camps and bases | An M4, Combat Shotgun or Bolt Action Rifle; Combat armor or a beret. | Shotgun shells, rifle bullets, iron, Tanned Leather. |
| Commando | Aircraft Carriers, silo guard and elite posts | An M4 and full Commando armor. | Steel, rubber, rifle bullets, Explosive Charges. |
| Dictator Dave (boss) | Red Military Bases | A Golden Revolver, a beret and a hundred hit points. | Always the Golden Revolver and 8 to 16 9mm bullets. |
| Camp Turret | Camps | An AK47 behind iron plating. Shoots you and your turrets. | Nothing; just break it. |
| Attack Helicopter | Boss: break a camp flag | Machine-gun bursts and rockets from the air. | 16 to 31 rifle bullets, 8 to 15 rockets, 2 or 3 Cybernetic Parts, 4 to 7 iron blocks. |
| Alien Bug | Bug nests | Fast, climbs walls, bites. Pours out of the monster spawners in desert and wasteland nests. | String and spider eyes. |

## Silo complexes

The guard spawners of an abandoned silo complex call up Army Soldiers. Rooms far from the hub hold elite spawners that call up Commandos and Stormtroopers. In a complex whose loot reaches LuV or higher, the elite posts are command posts that also call up the Outcast.

| Enemy | Carries | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stormtrooper | A silver Hazardous Environment suit and a Blaster or Laser Rifle. | Steel, rubber, redstone, circuits, batteries, and the parts of an energy gun: Laser Barrels and Focuses, Carbon Stocks and Receivers, tungstensteel plates and screws, titanium plates. |
| Outcast | Dark Power Armor and the heavy guns: Minigun, Tesla Rifle, Grim Reaper, Laser Rifle, Bio Gun, Flamethrower or LMG. | The richest table: rifle bullets, rockets, full Fuel and Bio Tanks, energy-gun parts, Mechanical Parts T3, Power Plating, Rhodium-Plated Palladium and tungstensteel plates, HSS-G ingots. |

## Ruins and bases

Chests hold loot up to the tier shown. Alien Bug Nests have no chests, only monster spawners. The Big Bunker's two spawners call up zombies. Every structure above ground stands on level, solid ground that is filled down to the natural ground, and the Military Base is one level pad with its roads, fence, tents and buildings.

- Small Factory, Train Station, House, Gas Station: LV
- Big Bunker: MV
- Military Base and camps: MV
- Aircraft Carrier, Submarine: MV

## What they drop

Up to two picks from their loot list, plus one more per level of Looting. Every NPC that carries a gun also drops rounds for it: a quarter to three quarters of a magazine, loose, one item per round. A Thompson gives 5 to 15 9mm bullets, and no stack is bigger than 64. Flame and bio guns drop one full tank. Energy guns drop the parts of their recipe, never the gun. A player kill has a small chance to drop the gun itself (2%, plus 1% per level of Looting; bosses never) and sometimes the armor they wear. Mobs Info lists the drops.

With Infernal Mobs installed, any of them can spawn with an Elite, Ultra or Infernal rank and better loot: LV items for Elite, MV for Ultra, and a rare HV drop from Infernal. The helicopter and camp turrets never do.

## How hard they hit

An NPC's gun damage follows the tier of the gun it fires: full damage up to MV, 85% at HV (the Attack Helicopter counts as HV), 70% at EV, 55% at IV, 45% at LuV and 40% at ZPM. Elite, Ultra and Infernal NPCs and bosses shoot at 75% on top of that, and NPC guns get half their armor penetration against players. NPCs aim true within 5 blocks; past that their aim error grows with distance, so almost every shot lands at 10 blocks, about half at 20 and about one in ten at 30. Gun and projectile hits do not knock their target back, for players and NPCs alike; explosions still throw. Your own guns and the Rampart turret are unchanged.

For a harder game, set every `combat.npcDamage*` key and `combat.npcEliteDamage` to 1.0, `damagefactors.armorPenetrationMultiplierNPCtoPlayer` to 1.0, `combat.npcSpreadPerBlock` to 0 and `combat.knockback` to true. All four together give every NPC gun its full stats.
