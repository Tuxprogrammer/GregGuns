# Enemies and loot

Who shoots back, where you will meet them, and what they carry and drop. Every faction can be switched off in the config. They do not fight each other, but they will shoot your turrets, and their guns hit for about a third of what yours do.

## In the wild

Natural spawns, Overworld only. The danger level rises the farther you travel from world spawn, and in harsh biomes: deserts, wastelands, frozen lands and swamps. Higher danger opens tougher spawns.

| Enemy | Danger | Carries | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zombie Farmer | 0+ | A hoe or a Hand Cannon, in Miner's clothes. | Wheat, seeds, leather, bread, apples, Heavy Cloth. |
| Zombie Miner | 0+ | A pickaxe or a Hand Cannon, and a Miner's Helmet. | Coal, redstone, iron, gunpowder, Heavy Cloth. |
| Zombie Soldier | 1+ | A revolver, a Thompson or a shovel, in Soldier armor. | Shotgun shells, 9mm bullets, iron, gunpowder, Heavy Cloth. |
| Skeleton Soldier | 1+ | A revolver, Thompson or Hand Cannon; a helmet and boots. | Bones, coal, gunpowder. |
| Psycho Steve | 1+, rare boss | A Chainsaw and red Miner armor. Very fast, very angry. | Always his Chainsaw and 4 to 8 Fuel Tanks. |
| Bandit | 2+ | A pistol, revolver, Sawed Off, Thompson, AK47 or Bolt Action Rifle, in Bandit armor. | Ammo, iron, gunpowder, Heavy Cloth. |

## At camps and nests

Military Camps and Aircraft Carriers fly a <ItemLink id="gregguns:camp_flag" /> that keeps calling in soldiers until you break it. Breaking it brings the helicopter.

| Enemy | Where | Carries | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Army Soldier | Camps | An M4, Combat Shotgun or Bolt Action Rifle; Combat armor or a beret. | Shotgun shells, rifle bullets, iron, Heavy Cloth. |
| Commando | Aircraft Carriers | An M4 and full Commando armor. | Steel, rubber, rifle bullets, Explosive Charges. |
| Camp Turret | Camps | An AK47 behind iron plating. Shoots you and your turrets. | Nothing; just break it. |
| Attack Helicopter | Boss: break a camp flag | Machine-gun bursts and rockets from the air. | 16 to 31 rifle bullets, 8 to 15 rockets, 2 or 3 Cybernetic Parts, 4 to 7 iron blocks. |
| Alien Bug | Bug nests | Fast, climbs walls, bites. Pours out of the monster spawners in desert and wasteland nests. | String and spider eyes. |

## Heavy hitters

These never spawn on their own. Your pack or server brings them in with the <ItemLink id="gregguns:npc_egg" showIcon="left" />, spawners or commands, for arenas, events and boss fights.

| Enemy | Carries | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Super Mutants (Basic, Heavy, Elite) | Each rank is bigger. A Rocket Launcher, AK47, Combat Shotgun or Laser Rifle. | Cybernetic Parts, blaze rods, ghast tears. |
| Stormtrooper | A silver Hazardous Environment suit and a Blaster or Laser Rifle. | Steel, rubber, redstone, circuits, batteries. |
| Outcast | Dark Power Armor and the heavy guns: Minigun, Tesla Rifle, Grim Reaper, Laser Rifle, Bio Gun, Flamethrower or LMG. | No loot of its own, but its gun and armor can drop. |
| Dictator Dave (boss) | A Golden Revolver, a beret and a hundred hit points. | Always the Golden Revolver and 8 to 16 9mm bullets. |
| Cyberdemon | Fireproof, with a Nether Blaster. | Cybernetic Parts, blaze rods, ghast tears. |
| Zombie Pigman Soldier | Combat armor and a Thompson, AK47, Flamethrower, LMG, Rocket Launcher or more. | Steel, gold, gunpowder, shotgun shells, rifle bullets. |

## Ruins and bases

Chests hold loot up to the tier shown. Alien Bug Nests have no chests, only monster spawners. The Big Bunker's two spawners call up zombies.

- Small Factory, Train Station, House, Gas Station: LV
- Big Bunker: MV
- Military Base and camps: MV
- Aircraft Carrier, Submarine: MV

## What they drop

Up to two picks from their loot list, plus one more per level of Looting. A player kill has a small chance to drop the gun they hold, and sometimes the armor they wear.

With Infernal Mobs installed, any of them can spawn with an Elite, Ultra or Infernal rank and better loot: LV items for Elite, MV for Ultra, and a rare HV drop from Infernal. The helicopter and camp turrets never do.
