# Armor and gear

Twelve armor sets from the Steam age to IV, plus masks, goggles and back gear for the equipment slots GregTech New Horizons already has. Worn-out armor protects nothing until it is repaired, so keep a Repair Bench handy. Powered perks need a charged chestplate; the base protection never does.

## Armor sets

- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t1_scout_chestplate" label="right" /> (Steam): **Bandit set.** Faster, jumps higher; light protection.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t1_miner_chestplate" label="right" /> (Steam): **Miner set.** Faster, and digs faster.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:steam_chestplate" label="right" /> (Steam): **Steam armor.** Bronze plates and cloth. Burn Coal or Charcoal in the chestplate for extra hearts, speed and a firmer stance; a Compressed Air Tank refills it too.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t1_combat_chestplate" label="right" /> (LV): **Soldier set.** Solid bullet protection, resists knockback.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t2_combat_chestplate" label="right" /> (MV): **Combat set.** Faster and tougher, in many camo patterns.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t2_commando_chestplate" label="right" /> (MV): **Commando set.** Better aim, digs fast underwater.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t2_riot_chestplate" label="right" /> (MV): **Ranger Veteran set.** Best at MV; shrugs off poison and radiation.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t3_combat_chestplate" label="right" /> (EV): **Advanced Combat set.** High protection, resists armor piercing.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t3_hev_chestplate" label="right" /> (EV): **Hazardous Environment suit.** Powered: fireproof, hazard-proof, big jumps.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t3_power_chestplate" label="right" /> (IV): **Power Armor.** Powered: the toughest set, extra hearts.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t3_exo_chestplate" label="right" /> (IV): **Exo Suit.** Powered: fast, light, soft landings.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:t2_beret" label="right" /> (MV): **Beret.** A hat with style, and 10% more speed.

## Masks, goggles and back gear

- <ItemImage id="gregguns:night_vision_goggles" label="right" /> (MV): Head slot, electric. Toggle with N.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:gas_mask" label="right" /> (MV): Blocks gas hazards, cures poison while the filter lasts.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:tactical_mask" label="right" /> (HV): Gas mask, night vision and oxygen in one.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:oxygen_mask" label="right" /> (HV): Breathe in space, together with the Oxygen Tanks. Refill the tanks with compressed air.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:oxygen_tanks" label="right" /> (HV): Back slot. Pairs with the Oxygen Mask.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:glider" label="right" /> (HV): Back slot. Hold J or sneak while falling to glide.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:jump_pack" label="right" /> (HV): Back slot, electric. Big jumps, soft landings.
- <ItemImage id="gregguns:anti_gravity_device" label="right" /> (IV): Back slot, electric. Press J, then fly.

## Extras

- <ItemLink id="gregguns:ammo_pouch" showIcon="left" />: quiver slot. Holds six stacks of ammo; open it with **P**.
- <ItemLink id="gregguns:bandages" showIcon="left" /> and <ItemLink id="gregguns:medkit" showIcon="left" />: heal on the spot, with Regeneration. Bandages at LV, the Medkit at MV.
- **Camo Bench:** cycles the camo on your gun and all four armor pieces. No power needed.

## Damage types

Ten damage types, and armor resists each one separately: Physical (melee, chainsaw, hammers), Projectile (bullets and pellets), Fire (flamethrower, blasters), Explosion (rockets, grenades, nukes), Energy (lasers and blasters), Poison (Bio Gun), Lightning (Tesla Rifle), Radiation (Nuclear Death Ray), Ice (resisted by some armor) and Unresistable (ignores armor).
