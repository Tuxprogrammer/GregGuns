# Armory Gantry

<ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20114" scale="2" label="right" />

The Repair Bench, built as a row of repair arches. It mends armor and guns from buses, ready for automation. The controller is an HV block.

**Size:** 7 wide, 7 tall and 7 to 19 long.

## Build it in sections

- **Booth**, once, at the front.
- **Repair bay**, 2 to 8 in a row.
- **Back wall**, once, at the back.

## Interior lamps

The booth and every repair bay need one lamp under the beam (5 in the 4-bay gantry). Use glowstone, a redstone lamp or any block with light level 12 or more. Without them the gantry will not form, so add lamps to gantries you built earlier.

## Hatches

- **Input Bus:** damaged items and repair parts go in.
- **Output Bus:** repaired items come out.
- **Energy and Maintenance Hatches:** on any Gantry Casing of the booth or the back wall.
- **No Muffler:** it makes no pollution.
- **The insides** are part of the build: a lamp in the booth and every bay, hoist motors and a gearbox, hooks, floor rails and a pipe run.

## Make it bigger

- **More bays:** one item per bay per cycle. A cycle takes about 2 seconds, plus up to 8 for badly damaged items.
- **A higher Energy Hatch:** the weakest Energy Hatch sets the highest item tier it repairs. LuV and ZPM gear needs a LuV or ZPM hatch.
- **Nothing lost:** repaired items keep their camo, charge, enchantments and names.

Hook it up to an ME system and it becomes a repair service for your whole base.
