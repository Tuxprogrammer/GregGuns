# Guns by tier

Every gun is built from GregTech parts in an Assembler, and each tier needs its gate material, so a gun arrives exactly when you can make it. Hold **Shift** over any gun to see its damage type, range, spread and magazine size.

Energy guns are their own batteries. Charge them in a GregTech Charger or Battery Buffer of exactly the gun's tier; a wrong-tier charger will not touch them.
