# Ammunition Works

<ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20105" scale="2" label="right" />

The Ammo Press, built as a production hall. It runs every Ammo Press recipe, many at once. The controller is an HV block.

**Size:** 5 wide, 7 tall and 13 to 31 long.

## Build it in sections

- **Feeder**, once, at the front.
- **Press bay**, 2 to 8 in a row.
- **Packing station**, once, at the back.

Use a hologram projector with a stack of 2 to 8 to preview the hall at that many bays.

## Interior lamps

Every feeder, press bay and the packing station needs one lamp under its roof (6 in the 4-bay hall). Use glowstone, a redstone lamp or any block with light level 12 or more. Without them the hall will not form, so add lamps to halls you built earlier.

## Hatches

- **Input Bus and Input Hatch:** on the feeder.
- **Energy, Maintenance and Muffler Hatches:** anywhere on the feeder. It does need a Muffler.
- **Output Bus and Output Hatch:** on the packing station's back wall.
- **The belt:** Item Pipe Casings down the middle of the floor, all of one tier.
- **The insides** are part of the build: pipe runs under the roof, a press rod and gearboxes in every bay, feed motors, an assembler arch and a lamp in every section. The glass windows show it running.

## Magazine filling bench (optional)

A 5 by 3 by 3 annex built against the packing station's lower back wall: Ammunition Works Casing around six tables in two rows of three. Natura's Workbench is the pick; a crafting table or Dire Crafting Table also works. It has its own Input Bus for empty magazines and its own Output Bus for loaded ones. Rounds come from the station's Output Bus, which must be a plain bus (an ME bus pushes its rounds away). The hall works without it.

## Make it bigger

- **More press bays:** parallels equal 4 times the bays times the voltage tier. At HV, 4 bays run 48 at once and 8 bays run 96.
- **A faster belt:** a higher-tier Item Pipe Casing belt shortens every run.
- **Bigger hatches:** power is the real limit. Feed a long hall with more amps or a higher-tier Energy Hatch.

A screwdriver toggles the ram animation.
