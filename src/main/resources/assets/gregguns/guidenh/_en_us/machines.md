# Machines

Every GregGuns machine starts as a single block. The ones you will run all day also come as a multiblock: same recipes, many at once. Start with the single blocks.

## Ammo Press

Steam to EV. Presses bullets, shells, rockets and magazines from GregTech parts. It has its own recipe tab in NEI.

<ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20100" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20101" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20102" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20103" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20104" label="right" />

Its multiblock is the [Ammunition Works](ammunition-works.md).

## Repair Bench

LV to IV. Restores worn armor and guns with GregTech plates and cloth. Its tier must be at least the item's.

<ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20109" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20110" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20111" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20112" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20113" label="right" />

Its multiblock is the [Armory Gantry](armory-gantry.md).

## Turret

MV to IV. Give it a gun, ammo and an armor plate. It guards your base and rebuilds itself after a hit.

<ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20116" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20117" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20118" label="right" /> <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20119" label="right" />

Its multiblock is the [Rampart](rampart.md).

## Camo Bench

LV. Swaps camo patterns on your gun and armor, one click per piece, so it never needs a bigger version. No power needed.

<ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20115" label="right" />

## Explosive Charges

MV and HV. Stick one on a wall, set depth and fuse, arm it and step back. It bores a clean tunnel and drops the blocks as items. The Advanced charge cuts obsidian.

<ItemImage id="gregguns:explosive_charge" label="right" />

## Storage and camp gear

Weapon and Reinforced Chests store your kit. Break an enemy camp's flag and an Attack Helicopter comes looking for you.
