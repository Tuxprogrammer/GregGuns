# Getting started

From a Steam-age hand cannon to a magazine-fed pistol, in six steps. The GregGuns questbook tab walks you up the tiers one weapon at a time.

## 1. Steam age: the Hand Cannon

Craft a <ItemLink id="gregguns:part_barrel_stone" showIcon="left" /> and a <ItemLink id="gregguns:part_stock_wooden" showIcon="left" />, then the <ItemLink id="gregguns:handcannon" showIcon="left" />. It fires one <ItemLink id="gregguns:ammo_stone_bullet" /> per shot. Make bullets by hand, or 24 at a time in a Steam Ammo Press.

<ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20100" label="right" />

## 2. LV: your first pistol

Guns are put together from GregTech parts in an Assembler. Each tier needs its gate material: **Steel** for LV, Aluminum for MV, Stainless Steel for HV. For the <ItemLink id="gregguns:pistol_9mm" showIcon="left" /> you need a <ItemLink id="gregguns:part_barrel_hardened" /> and <ItemLink id="gregguns:part_mechanical_2" />.

## 3. Build an Ammo Press

<ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20101" label="right" />

The press has its own recipe tab in NEI. A press runs every recipe at or below its tier: the Steam press runs LV recipes too, and higher tiers overclock as usual.

## 4. Press rounds and magazines

Brass foil, lead rounds and gunpowder become <ItemLink id="gregguns:ammo_9mm_bullet" />. Plates, springs and Mechanical Parts become empty magazines. Fill them in the press, or craft one <ItemLink id="gregguns:mag_9mm_pistol" /> with 3 bullets.

## 5. Reload and fight

Press **R** to swap in a loaded magazine. The empty one comes back to your inventory, so refill it and go again. Shoot the soldiers, bandits and mutants for loot.

## 6. Repair, then climb the tiers

Worn armor and guns go back on a Repair Bench of at least their tier, with GregTech plates and cloth.

<ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20109" label="right" />

## Default keys

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left mouse | Fire (hold for auto) |
| Right mouse | Scope, or charge a beam |
| R | Reload |
| N | Night vision |
| P | Ammo pouch |
| J | Glider or anti-gravity |
