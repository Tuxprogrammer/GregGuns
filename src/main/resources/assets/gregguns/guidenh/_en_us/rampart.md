# Rampart

<ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20120" scale="2" label="right" />

The Turret, built as a battlement. Up to ten turrets share one ammo feed and one power feed. The controller is an HV block.

**Size:** 6 tall, 3 deep and 8 to 24 long.

## Build it in sections

- **Gatehouse**, once, at one end.
- **Wall bay with a mount**, 2 to 10 in a row.
- **End cap**, once, at the other end.

## Hatches

- **Input Bus:** the shared ammo store for every mount (ME works).
- **Output Bus:** empty magazines come back here.
- **Energy and Maintenance Hatches:** on the gatehouse plating.
- **Gun Mount Hatch:** exactly one on each bay's sill. Right-click it with a gun to arm it. <ItemImage id="gregtech:gt.blockmachines:20121" label="right" />

A mount takes any GregGuns gun up to its own tier, except akimbo pairs. Right-click it empty-handed to take the gun back.

## Make it bigger

- **More mounts:** another turret every two blocks of wall, on the same feed.
- **Better plating:** every mount gets the armor of the weakest turret armor plate used: <ItemLink id="gregguns:turret_armor_iron" /> 5, <ItemLink id="gregguns:turret_armor_steel" /> 10, <ItemLink id="gregguns:turret_armor_aluminum" /> 15, <ItemLink id="gregguns:turret_armor_carbon" /> 20.
- **Security:** a screwdriver cycles who it spares: everyone, your friends, or only you. Friends are the members and allies of your ServerUtilities team.
