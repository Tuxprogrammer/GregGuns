# M4: clean-room model notes

Design notes for rebuilding the Techguns **M4 assault rifle** look-alike in GregGuns. Written by the "dirty room" analyst from study of the original's model, renderer and textures. They are meant to be complete enough to build from without ever seeing the original. They contain no source, no box lists, no field names and no texture coordinates; the builder designs its own UV layout. The magazine is covered only as far as it shows on the gun. The M4 Infiltrator variant is covered in the closing "Infiltrator delta" section.

Related facts: [ModelM4](../gun-models.md#modelm4-128x64-family-z-42-boxes-1-part), [M4 sheet](../gun-sheets.md#m4-m4), [rendering framework](../rendering-framework.md), [Techguns M4 stats](../../02-guns/gun-catalog.md#table-a-identity-and-ammunition).

**Units and axes used in this document.** One unit = one texel at the native 128x64 scale = 1/16 block. Muzzle toward **+X**. **Y points down** (Minecraft model convention), with **Y = 0 at the top of the red-dot sight's roof**, the highest point of the gun. **Z = 0 is the gun's mid-plane**; **+Z is the shooter's left side, -Z the shooter's right side** (the side with the forward assist). Sizes across Z are total width, centered unless a Z range is given. **X = 0 is the rear face of the buttpad**, so the muzzle is near X = 69. Rounded to the nearest half unit unless it matters.

## 1. Gestalt

A dark, matte, **tactical black carbine** in the AR-15 / M4A1 mold, drawn with chunky Minecraft-scale boxes. At a glance you read: a long horizontal receiver with a **flat-top rail**; a boxy **holographic red-dot sight** perched on top with a visible window; a **quad-rail handguard** (rails on all four sides, with slotted details) ending in a tall A-frame front sight and a thin barrel stub; a **vertical foregrip** hanging under the handguard; a **raked pistol grip**; a **light-gray ribbed magazine** that is the only bright thing on the gun; and a **collapsible stock** shaped like a wedge at the back. Everything else is near-black blue-gray steel and polymer.

Defining features in order of visual importance:

1. **All-black body with a light-gray magazine.** One strong contrast: the mag. The rest is a family of very dark blue-grays.
2. **Red-dot sight on a tall base.** It stands well above the rail; its roof, two side ears and open window make a distinct square hood, with one red pixel block inside.
3. **Quad-rail handguard**, roughly as long as the receiver, with notched slots along its rails, ending in the front-sight A-frame and a thin diamond barrel.
4. **Vertical foregrip** (a diamond-section post) below the handguard, set far forward of the magazine.
5. **Collapsible stock**: a thick buffer tube on top, a vertical buttpad at the rear, and a slanted strut underneath, forming a triangular wedge.
6. **Raked pistol grip and flat trigger plank** forming a clear gap in front of the grip.

## 2. Overall proportions

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Total length (buttpad rear to muzzle) | about 69 |
| Total height (sight roof to magazine bottom) | about 24 |
| Total thickness (rails across the handguard) | 6 (body is 3 to 4) |
| Length : height : thickness | about 11.5 : 4 : 1 |
| Barrel stub beyond the handguard | 18, about 26 percent of the total length |
| Handguard length | 15 (X 36 to 51), about the same as the receiver (16) |
| Receiver (upper, with rear plates) | X 16 to 34, 18 long |
| Stock (buttpad to tube front) | 15 long, about 22 percent of the total length |
| Magazine | 2 wide across Z, 6 long along X, about 8 tall below the magwell |
| Bore axis | about Y = 8, the center of the receiver and handguard |

How the pieces sit along the length, from the rear: the stock tube and buttpad (X 0 to 15); a 1-long bolt and charging-handle zone and rear receiver plates (X 15 to 18); the receiver body (X 18 to 34); a diamond gas-block collar (X 34 to 36); the quad-rail handguard (X 36 to 51); then the front sight at X 52 to 54 and the barrel running out to X 69.

**Vertical layers (top to bottom at the middle of the gun):** sight roof Y 0; sight body top Y 3; rear-sight top Y 4; top rail Y 5; receiver top Y 6; receiver bottom Y 10; lower receiver bottom Y 13; magwell bottom Y 16; mag bottom Y 24. The pistol grip bottom reaches Y 21. The foregrip bottom reaches Y 19.5. The buttpad bottom reaches Y 18.

The **top line is stepped, not flat**: the red-dot sight rises 5 above the rail; the rear sight pokes 1 above the rail; the rails of the receiver and handguard are level; only the front sight rises again, to Y 2, which is 3 units above the rail.

## 3. Part by part

All parts are boxes. Z widths are centered on the mid-plane unless noted. "Touches" means faces in contact or overlapping.

### Barrel
- A square bar 2x2 in section and **18 long**, turned 45 degrees about its axis so it shows a **diamond**: edge-on, about 2.8 units tall and wide, with its center at Y about 8.
- X 51 to 69. The rear 1 unit may be hidden inside the handguard front. No taper, no flash hider, no crown, no notches. One clean bar.
- Touches: buried in the handguard's front face; the front sight's brace sits on it just ahead of the handguard.

### Gas-block collar
- A **diamond-section** block, 4x4 in section turned 45 degrees (about 5.7 corner to corner), **2 long** (X 34 to 36), centered on the bore axis (Y about 7.8).
- It sits between the receiver's front face and the handguard. It is **taller than the receiver** (it pokes about 1 above the receiver's top and bottom) and sticks out about 1.2 to each side of the receiver. From the front it reads as a black diamond hiding the muzzle's root; from the side, as a thin tall slab at the joint.

### Handguard (quad rail)
- **Core:** 4 wide, 4 tall, **15 long** (X 36 to 51, Y 6 to 10).
- **Four rails, each 13 long** (X 37 to 50), set 1 unit back from each end of the core:
  - top rail: 2 wide, 1 tall, on top (Y 5 to 6);
  - bottom rail: 2 wide, 1 tall, below (Y 10 to 11);
  - two side rails: 1 wide, 2 tall, one on each flank (Y 7 to 9), at Z of magnitude 2 to 3.
- The cross-section reads as a **plus sign**: 6 wide, 6 tall overall (Y 5 to 11), with the core in the middle. The rails have black slot notches along their length (painted, section 5).
- Touches: the core's rear end touches the collar; the top rail is level with the receiver's top rail.

### Front sight (A-frame)
- A 1-wide post, **3 tall** (Y 4 to 7), at X 53 to 54, standing on the barrel just ahead of the handguard.
- A **hood block** on top: 2 long, 2 tall, 1 wide (X 52 to 54, Y 2 to 4), the highest point of the front half.
- A **slanted brace**: a 1x4 strip leaning back about 25 degrees from the hood's rear toward the handguard front, from about (X 52.5, Y 3) down to about (X 51, Y 6.5). It ties the sight to the handguard so the sight reads as a triangular A-frame.
- Everything is 1 wide, centered. No notch, no ring.

### Vertical foregrip
- **Mount:** a block 4 long, 2 tall, 3 wide (X 39.5 to 43.5, Y 9.5 to 11.5) clamped on the underside of the handguard, overlapping the bottom rail.
- **Post:** a bar 2x2 in section and **8 tall**, turned 45 degrees about the vertical axis, so it reads as a **diamond-section post** about 2.8 across. It hangs from Y 11.5 to 19.5, centered at X about 41.5.
- It is **vertical** (no rake) and sits about 10 units in front of the magazine's front face.

### Upper receiver
- **Main block:** 4 wide, 4 tall, **16 long** (X 18 to 34, Y 6 to 10).
- **Lower receiver:** 3 wide, 3 tall, **15 long** (X 18 to 33, Y 10 to 13), directly under the upper block. The body therefore steps in slightly from 4 wide to 3 wide, a visible shoulder on each side.
- **Rear plates**, behind the main block (X 16 to 18): an upper end plate 4 wide, 3 tall (Y 7 to 10), and a lower end plate 3 wide, 1 tall (Y 10 to 11).
- **Charging handle** at the rear top (X 16 to 18, Y 6 to 7): a 4-wide, 1-tall, 1-long T-bar at X 16 to 17 on a 2-wide stem at X 17 to 18. It pokes over the rear plate's top and sticks out about 0.5 past each side of the upper block.
- **Top rail:** 2 wide, 1 tall, **15 long**, on top of the main block (X 18.5 to 33.5, Y 5 to 6). Slot marks painted (section 5).
- **Rear sight block:** 3 wide, 2 tall, 4 long (X 19 to 23, Y 4 to 6), sitting on the top rail at the rear. It reads as a carry-handle stub with painted dark-red aperture marks. It is 1 above the rail and does not touch the red-dot sight (a 1-unit gap).
- **Forward assist:** a thin **1x1 bar, 4 long**, lying on the **shooter's right side** (-Z side) of the receiver at about Y 7 (Y 7.2 to 8.2). It runs from X 17 at Z about -3, angled about **15 degrees** toward the centerline, to X 21 at Z about -2 (so its front end is buried in the receiver's side face). It sticks out about 1 unit at the rear and is the only side-mounted detail. Dark, the same shade as the receiver.

### Red-dot (holographic) sight
- **Base:** 4 wide, 3 tall, **9 long** (X 24 to 33, Y 3 to 6), straddling the top rail. It is a solid dark block, the widest piece of the upper body, and it looks flush with the receiver's width (both 4).
- **Roof:** 4 wide, 1 tall, 4 long (X 25 to 29, Y 0 to 1), the top of the whole gun.
- **Two side ears:** each 1 wide, 2 tall, 4 long (X 25 to 29, Y 1 to 3), one on each side at Z of 1.5 to 2.5 in magnitude (the outer edges flush with the roof's edges).
- Between the ears is an **open window**: 2 wide, 2 tall, 4 long (X 25 to 29, Y 1 to 3), see-through from front and back as a dark square tunnel with the base's top as its floor.
- **Red dot:** a 1x1 plane facing along the gun's axis, in the middle of the window (X about 27, Y 1.5 to 2.5, centered across Z). It reads as a bright red square from the front and the back, and is invisible from the side.
- The sight is **offset forward** of the rear sight and sits over the middle of the receiver. Its front 1 unit and its rear 1 unit stick out past the window, so the window sits in the back two thirds of the base.

### Pistol grip
- A slab 3 wide, 4 long (front to back), **9 tall**, top at Y 13 (under the lower receiver, X 18 to 22), **raked back about 25 degrees** about its top. The bottom ends at about Y 21, X 14 to 18: the grip's bottom is about 4 behind its top.
- It is a single straight tilted slab. The grip's texture has a checkered pattern (section 5).
- Touches: its top face is buried in the lower receiver's underside.

### Trigger and guard plank
- **Trigger:** a 1 wide, 2 tall, 1 long block (X 22 to 23, Y 12.5 to 14.5), tilted about 15 degrees with the tip back, in the pocket behind the magwell.
- **Guard plank:** a flat bar 2 wide, 1 tall, **5 long** (X 20 to 25, Y 15 to 16), running from the grip's front to the magwell's rear. It is the bottom of the guard. There is no loop and no front post. In side view the guard is a thin horizontal line closing a pocket 4 long by 2 tall in front of the grip.

### Magazine well and magazine (as seen on the gun)
- **Magwell:** 3 wide, 3 tall, **8 long** (X 25 to 33, Y 13 to 16), under the lower receiver, the same width as it.
- **Magazine:** light gray, 2 wide, 6 long (X 26.5 to 32.5). The upper part drops from Y 16 to Y 21 (5 tall); the lower part (Y 20 to 24) is 4 tall and tilted about 9 degrees so its bottom end swings **forward** by about 0.6, a slight banana curve. In the model the magazine's top is tucked flush against the magwell bottom, so no cartridge or rim shows on the gun.
- The magazine is the lowest point of the gun (Y 24). It is thinner (2) than the well (3) and about 1.5 shorter front to back at each end (a 1-unit ledge shows front and rear of the mag where it meets the well).
- Not on the gun: the empty-magazine and full-magazine item models (a yellow cartridge top and a top slab) and their inventory render. Do not model them here.

### Stock (collapsible, wedge-shaped)
All pieces are centered; widths are across Z.
- **Buffer tube:** 3 wide, 3 tall, **15 long** (X 0 to 15, Y 7 to 10). The tube's front end meets the receiver's rear plates (a 1-unit gap at X 15 to 16 is filled by the small plate below). A small diamond-section (45 degree) castle nut, 2x2 section, 1 long, sits at X 15 to 16, centered at the tube's front end, joining it to the receiver.
- **Buttpad:** 2 wide, **8 tall**, 2 long (X 0 to 2, Y 10 to 18), vertical, at the very rear, hanging **below** the tube's end.
- **Rear web:** 1 wide, 5 tall, 3 long (X 1 to 4, Y 9 to 14), a thin plate bridging tube and buttpad.
- **Tube web:** 1 wide, 4 tall, **8 long** (X 4 to 12, Y 9 to 13), a thin fin hanging below the tube's middle, 3 units below the tube's floor.
- **Front bulkhead:** 2 wide, 3 tall, 1 long (X 12 to 13, Y 10 to 13), at the tube's front.
- **Rear strut:** 2 wide, 9 long, leaning back about **34 degrees** from vertical, from the tube's underside at about X 5 to 7, Y 10, down and back to about X 0 to 2, Y 17.5, where it meets the buttpad's bottom. It forms the lower rear edge of the wedge.
- **Front strut:** 2 wide, 4 long, leaning back about **60 degrees** from vertical, starting at about X 10 to 12, Y 13, ending about X 6.5 to 8.5, Y 15, with a 2x2x2 joint block at X 7 to 9, Y 13 to 15, and a 2x2x2 block at X 10 to 12, Y 11 to 13 above it.
- The wedge reads as: horizontal tube across the top, vertical pad at the back, a diagonal from tube to pad's foot, with a smaller second diagonal and fin pieces inside. See section 10 on how these pieces link.

**Shading style:**
- **Flat, per-pixel, little noise.** Large faces are one value with 1 to 3 stray darker or lighter texels. No gradients, no scratches, no wear. The overall look is very dark: the whole gun except the magazine sits between #111216 and #2F333E, with only the barrel (#414141) a little lighter.
- **Rails and slots:** the top rail and the handguard rails carry **repeating pure-black slot marks** (about 1 black texel in 2 along the length), reading as Picatinny cross-slots. They are the most visible surface detail.
- **Pistol grip:** a diagonal **checker or grip-texture pattern** in #21242C and #2E323C, visible on both flanks.
- **Magazine:** ribbed, with a repeating light/mid/dark banding of #606060 / #515151 / #414141 across its flanks. This is the single bright element.
- **Sight:** the window's inner surfaces are the darkest values (#111216). The red dot is the only saturated color, and a thin line of the same red can appear on the hood's inner face.
- **Barrel and collar:** the barrel is lighter (#414141 on the top and sides), with a darker lower face; the collar is darker (#1E2027).

## 6. In-game presentation

All transforms apply to the model as a whole, in block units after the model's 1/16 scale. The model is first rotated 180 degrees about X (Y-down becomes screen-up). Translations shown are applied after the rotation and scale.

**Muzzle flash point:** the front of the barrel on its axis (model X about 69, Y about 8, Z = 0). A flash sprite at 1.5 times scale, with a small muzzle particle. In third person the flash is drawn slightly forward of the hand at about (0, 0.025, 0).

**Timing:** fire delay 3 ticks (full auto), recoil animation 5 ticks, muzzle flash 5 ticks, reload 45 ticks (2.25 s), clip 30.

## 7. Motion

- The **model has no moving parts.** The bolt, charging handle, magazine and trigger do not move. All motion is whole-gun transforms.
- **Recoil:** first person, a light pitch kick and a small shift back (see section 6). Third person: the gun pitches up about 15 degrees about its origin in roughly the first quarter of the first half of the animation, then settles back.
- **Reload:** 45 ticks. First person: the gun tips and the barrel dips, hangs, and returns. Third person: about 30 degrees pitch plus 40 degrees yaw about a pivot near the hand, at mid-animation, then returns.
- **Optional (not in the original):** the builder may keep the magazine, charging handle and trigger as separate named parts so later code can animate them, but the rest pose must match this document.

## 8. What to avoid

- **A bright or colorful body.** The original is almost black. Only the magazine is light, and the red dot is the only saturated color.
- **A smooth, flat top.** Do not flatten the stepped top (optic, rear sight, rails, front sight). The red-dot sight is a tall box on a base and must stand 5 units above the rail.
- **An open or hollow handguard.** It is a solid 4x4 core with four rails (a plus sign), not an empty tube.
- **A thick or tapered barrel, a muzzle brake or a suppressor.** The M4's barrel is a bare 2.8-wide diamond stub, 18 long. (The silencer belongs to the Infiltrator only.)
- **A loop trigger guard.** The original has a flat plank only.
- **A fat or straight-up pistol grip.** It is 3 wide and raked about 25 degrees.
- **A forward-raked or very thick magazine.** The mag is 2 wide and light gray, its lower end curving slightly forward.
- **A missing or merged stock.** The stock has a tube, a vertical pad and diagonal struts; it is a wedge, not a solid block.
- **Adding what the original lacks:** no scope, no laser, no suppressor, no side rail attachments, no sling, no bipod, no ejection port cover.
- **Showing the magazine's top or a cartridge.** It is hidden inside the magwell.
- **Floating bits.** Every sight, rail and strut touches or overlaps a neighbor.

## 10. Unsure

- **Stock internals.** The thin fins, the small blocks and the two angled struts overlap in a way that is hard to read; I understand them as the buffer tube, a rear web, a tube web and two struts, but how the second (front) strut ties to the first is not clear. The wedge's outline (tube on top, vertical pad at the back, long diagonal underneath) is certain; the interior fill is approximate. A builder may simplify the interior as long as the outline and the hollow triangle survive.
- **Forward assist side and role.** I read the angled bar as a forward assist on the shooter's right. Its exact tip position is approximate (within about 0.5 unit).
- **Front-sight brace.** The leaning strip's lower end is estimated at the handguard's front face; it may be 0.5 unit off.
- **Sight window.** The window's depth (4 along X) and the dot's exact X are derived from the sizes, not seen directly; the dot could sit 0.5 unit off the middle.
- **First-person framing numbers** come from formulas read, not seen in game. Treat the offsets and angles as approximate.
- **Camo colors** are measured from the receiver, handguard and stock regions; texture noise in the originals is higher than the summary suggests.

## Infiltrator delta (`m4_uq`, "The Infiltrator")

Same skeleton, same proportions, same lower half (stock, magazine, grip, trigger, magwell, receiver, handguard rails, collar). One texture only (no camo). Everything below is a **change** to the M4 above.

**Removed:** the red-dot sight's roof, ears, window and red dot; the 4-wide, 9-long sight base.

**Added or changed:**
- **Scope in place of the red-dot sight** (the Infiltrator's signature):

## Round 2 corrections (orchestrator review)

These facts come from comparing the first GregGuns build (B1 round 1) against the original, in the same axes as above (X 0 at the buttpad, Y 0 at the red-dot roof, Z 0 at the mid-plane, -Z the shooter's right). The first build matched the part layout to within 0.5 unit almost everywhere: receiver, optic, handguard, barrel, foregrip and magwell. Silhouette overlap was 0.93 from the side, 0.91 from the top, but only 0.83 from the front and back. **One part causes nearly all of the front, back and top loss.**

**Overall size to hit:** 69 long, 24.9 tall (roof to the lowest magazine corner), **6.0 wide** (Z -3.0 to +3.0). The widest parts are the two handguard side rails, whose outer faces sit exactly 3.0 from the mid-plane. Nothing else is wider.

### Forward assist (the cause of the front, back and top error)

The section above has its shape right (4 long, 1 x 1 in section, angled 15.4 degrees, Y 7.2 to 8.2, on the shooter's right, from X 17 to about X 21), but round 1 set it too far out. Its **outermost face must sit at Z -3.0, flush with the outer faces of the handguard's side rails**, and only at its rear end (X 17). Measured from the mid-plane: the rear end's outer face is 3.0 out (the receiver's flank is at 2.0, so it stands 1.0 proud there), and the bar then runs forward and inward until its inner face is flush with the flank at X about 21. Round 1 reached 3.5 out, 0.5 beyond everything else. That made the gun 6.5 wide, pushed the bounding center 0.25 off the gun's axis, and shifted every part's overlap in the front, back and top views. Removing that one bar in a test render lifted front overlap from 0.82 to 0.95 and top overlap from 0.88 to 0.99. Keep the bar with its outermost reach at exactly 3.0, dark like the receiver (#1D1F26).

### Lowest point of the magazine

The model is 24.9 tall; round 1 is 24.4. The lower magazine segment is 4 tall, 6 long, 2 wide (X 26.4 to 32.4 at the top, Y 20 to 24 before the lean). It is **hinged on the top of its front edge** (X 32.4, Y 20) and tipped 9 degrees so its bottom swings forward by 0.6. Because it hinges at the front, the bottom edge is a sloping line: the front-bottom corner is at Y 24.0 and the **rear-bottom corner is the lowest point of the gun at Y 24.9**. Round 1 hinged it near the middle, so its rear corner stopped at Y 24.4. The upper segment above it is unchanged: 5 tall (Y 16 to 21), 6 long, vertical.

### Stock struts hang too low

The slanted pair under the stock tube should read as a shallow triangular web that stays above the line of the buttpad's foot. The rear strut is 2 thick and 9 long, hinged at its top at X 7, Y 10, tipped 33.9 degrees so it runs back and down to end at about X 2, Y 17.5, beside the buttpad. The front strut is a short 2 x 4 piece hinged at X 12, Y 13, tipped 60 degrees so it runs rearward and only **slightly downward**: its tip ends at about X 8.5, Y 15.0, a shallow 30-degree slope. Round 1's front strut ended about 0.8 lower and 1.0 further forward, leaving a small wedge hanging under the web. Shorten or re-angle it to end at that point.

### Paint (from the round 1 sheet)

The round 1 palette was close in hue but a little flat. The original's main mid-tone face color is **#272A33** (about 23 percent of the texture); round 1 used it for under 10 percent and leaned on #23252C and #1B1D23 instead, so the whole gun read about 6 levels too dark. Target the original's mix: base #272A33 for receiver, rail and handguard faces; shadow #22242C and #1D1F26 for undersides and the sides of recesses; deepest #121317 (about a quarter of the texture) for the bolt, charging handle and the 1-unit gaps; pure #000000 only inside the optic window. The magazine is neutral light gray (about #606060) with a darker rib pattern and no brown or beige tint in any texel.
