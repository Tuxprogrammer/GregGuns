# AS50: clean-room model notes

Design notes for rebuilding the Techguns **AS50 Sniper Rifle** (a very long, near-black, scoped anti-materiel rifle) look-alike in GregGuns. Written by the "dirty room" analyst from study of the original's model, renderer and texture. They are meant to be complete enough to build from without ever seeing the original. They contain no source, no box lists, no field names and no texture coordinates; the builder designs its own UV layout. The magazine is covered as far as it shows on the gun.

Related facts: [ModelAS50](../gun-models.md#modelas50-256128-family-z-50-boxes-1-part), [AS50 sheet](../../02-guns/sheets-rifles-shotguns.md#as50-sniper-rifle-as50), [rendering framework](../rendering-framework.md), [Techguns AS50 stats](../../02-guns/gun-catalog.md#table-a-identity-and-ammunition).

**Units and axes used in this document** (the same convention as the SCAR notes). One unit = 1/16 block = one texel at the native 256x128 texture scale. Muzzle toward **+X**. **Y points down** (Minecraft model convention), with **Y = 0 at the top of the scope's elevation turret**, the highest point of the gun. **Z = 0 is the gun's mid-plane**; **+Z is the shooter's left side, -Z the shooter's right side**. Sizes across Z are total width, centered unless a Z range is given. **X = 0 is the rear face of the buttpad**, so the muzzle tip is at X = 100. Rounded to a tenth of a unit where it matters, otherwise to the nearest half unit. "Diamond" means a square bar turned 45 degrees about the gun's long axis, so it shows a point at top and bottom and a point on each side; the across-corners width of a square of side s is 1.41 s (2 gives 2.83, 3 gives 4.24, 4 gives 5.66, 5 gives 7.07). Angles are described as "tilt" with the direction stated in words (X-Y side view, muzzle on the right).

## 1. Gestalt

A big, long, **matte-black anti-materiel sniper rifle** in the Barrett/AS50 mold, drawn with chunky boxes and diamond-section bars. At a glance you read: one extremely long straight line from a tall flat buttpad to a fat slotted muzzle brake; a **big black telescopic scope** riding high on a rail, with a fat objective bell at the front, an eyepiece at the rear and two dial turrets; a **thick diamond-section barrel shroud** in front of the receiver that carries a floating ribbed rail on top; a long, thin, **bare barrel** with two small collars; and a **box magazine hanging in front of the trigger** rather than behind it. Below the receiver sit a steeply raked pistol grip and a closed trigger guard. The gun is **almost all black** (#1E1E1E), with only mid-dark-gray accents. There is no wood, no tan and no color accent anywhere, apart from a faint indigo tint in the scope glass.

Defining features in order of visual importance:

1. **Extreme length and low profile.** The gun is about 3.4 times as long as it is tall, with a long, straight, horizontal top line. Barrel plus shroud together cover half the gun.
2. **A large scope floating high above the receiver**, with a visibly bigger objective bell (7 across) at the front, a smaller tube in the middle and a medium eyepiece at the rear. A deep slot of air lies between scope and receiver.
3. **Diamond-section barrel parts**: a fat 5.7-across shroud, a thin 2.8-across barrel, and a fat 5.7-across multi-baffle muzzle brake. The shroud and brake are the same width, and they bracket the thin barrel.
4. **Box magazine ahead of the trigger**, long, narrow and the lightest-colored piece on the gun.
5. **Cheek rest and tall buttpad**: a diamond-section comb above the stock and a 10-tall pad at the rear.
6. **A steeply raked pistol grip** with a closed trigger guard.

## 3. Part by part

All parts are boxes or turned boxes (diamonds). "Touches" means faces in contact or overlapping. Y values are measured from the top of the elevation turret. X values are measured from the buttpad.

### Buttpad
- Two thin plates stacked front to rear. The rear plate is **5 wide, 10 tall, 1 thick** (X 0 to 1). The front plate is **4 wide, 10 tall, 1 thick** (X 1 to 2). Both span Y 12.5 to 22.5.
- Together they give a stepped 2-thick pad: wider at the very back. Seen from behind it is a tall upright rectangle, 5 by 10.
- The pad is the tallest part of the stock. Its top (Y 12.5) is 3 below the cheek rest's crest (9.5); its bottom (Y 22.5) matches the stock's lowest piece.

### Stock body
- A solid block, **4 wide, 5 tall, 14 long** (X 2 to 16, Y 15.5 to 20.5), directly behind the receiver's rear face (its front face meets the receiver's rear face at X 16). Its top (Y 15.5) is 2 above the receiver's bottom edge (Y 17.5) and 4 below the receiver's top, so the stock sits low; its bottom is flush with the trigger group's bottom (Y 20.5).
- It reads as a plain slab with no cut-outs, no thumbhole, no hooks.

### Cheek rest
- A diamond bar: a **4 by 4 square bar, 16 long, turned 45 degrees**, so it is 5.7 across and 5.7 tall (X 0 to 16, Y 9.5 to 15.2, Z -2.8 to 2.8).
- It lies along the top of the stock body and runs the full length of the stock, from the pad's back face to the receiver's rear face. The crest point (Y 9.5) is the highest point of the rear half.
- The diamond's lowest corner (Y 15.2) lies 0.3 above the stock body's top face. Overlap them by at least 0.5 unit in your build so the cheek rest does not hover; the original's gap is imperceptible.
- Seen from the side it is a flat-topped, long slab between the pad and the receiver cap, 5.7 tall; from the top it is a long strip 5.7 wide, which is wider than the 4-wide stock body (so it overhangs 0.9 each side).

### Stock underplate ("flip")
- A thin plate **3 wide, 2 tall, 10 long** (X 2 to 12, Y 20.5 to 22.5) under the stock body, right behind the buttpad. It brings the underside of the rear stock down to the pad's bottom (Y 22.5).
- It is shorter than the stock (ends at X 12), leaving a 4-long, 2-deep step at X 12 to 16 before the trigger group.
- It is a plain block, not a monopod, and it does not move.

### Receiver
- Main body: **4 wide, 6 tall, 32 long** (X 16 to 48, Y 11.5 to 17.5). A plain slab with a recessed ejection-port look painted on the sides (section 5). No real cut-out.
- Cap: a **3 wide, 1 tall, 32 long** strip (X 16 to 48, Y 10.5 to 11.5) on top, narrower by 0.5 each side, giving a small step.
- Rail: **2 wide, 2 tall, 25 long** (X 22 to 47, Y 8.5 to 10.5) on the cap, narrower still by 0.5 each side. The rail's top (Y 8.5) is the datum line for the scope mount.
- Touches: the stock body at the rear; the shroud at the front (X 48); the trigger group below; the scope mount above.
- Stack summary seen from the front: 4 wide (receiver), 3 wide (cap), 2 wide (rail), 3 wide (scope mount), then the scope. A stepped, tower-like silhouette.

### Trigger group
- Upper housing: **4 wide, 3 tall, 17 long** (X 16 to 33, Y 17.5 to 20.5), under the receiver's rear 17 units. It has the receiver's width, so the underside of the receiver continues smoothly into it.
- Lower keel: **3 wide, 1 tall, 13 long** (X 18 to 31, Y 20.5 to 21.5) under the housing. It lies 2 units in from the housing's rear and 2 units short of its front.
- The magazine holder starts exactly at the housing's front end (X 32 to 33).

### Pistol grip
- A box **3 wide, 9 long, 4 thick**, tilted **26 degrees** about the cross-gun axis so that the grip leans **back** (the bottom is toward the stock). It is the longest and most visible tilted part.
- Front face: runs from a top at about (X 27, Y 21.5) to a bottom at about (X 23.1, Y 29.6). Rear face: from (X 23.4, Y 19.8) to (X 19.5, Y 27.9). The bottom face is thus slanted by the same angle, with the front-bottom corner the lowest point of the whole gun (Y 29.6).
- The top end tucks into the trigger housing (overlapping its lower half). Grip thickness measured perpendicular to the lean is 4, and 3 across Z.
- Seen from the side it is a narrow, strongly raked slab with a clear lean (about the same as an AK grip). It is the lowest part of the gun, 1.1 below the magazine's bottom.

### Trigger guard and trigger
- Floor plate: **2 wide, 1 tall, 6 long** (X 25 to 31, Y 23.5 to 24.5). Its rear end sinks into the grip's front face.
- Front post: **2 wide, 2 tall, 1 long** (X 30 to 31, Y 21.5 to 23.5), joining the floor's front end to the keel's underside (Y 21.5).
- So the guard is a closed loop of grip (rear), keel (top), post (front) and floor (bottom), with an opening about 4 long (X 26 to 30) and 2 tall (Y 21.5 to 23.5).
- Trigger blade: **1 wide, 2 tall, 1 thick**, in the middle of the opening (X 27 to 28.5, Y 21 to 23.2), tilted 15 degrees so that its lower end swings forward. It hangs from the keel.

### Magazine holder and magazine
- Holder (the housing around the magazine well): **3 wide, 4 tall, 13 long** (X 32 to 45.3, Y 15.5 to 20.6), tilted 5 degrees so that its rear end sits about 1 unit lower than its front. It overlaps the receiver's lower edge and the housing's front.
- Magazine body: **2 wide, 8 tall, 12 long** (X 32.5 to 44.5, Y 19.5 to 27.5). It is 1 narrower than the holder on each side, so the holder looks like a 3-wide rim around a 2-wide magazine with a visible step; the body's lower 7 units hang below the holder.
- Magazine floor plate: **2 wide, 2 tall, 11 long** (X 33 to 44.2, Y 25.5 to 28.5), tilted 5 degrees with the same sense as the holder (rear lower than front, so it drops 1 unit from front to rear). It extends the magazine downward to Y 28.5 and gives its bottom a faint rake.
- Base tab: a **2 by 2 by 1** block (X 32.5 to 33.5, Y 26.5 to 28.5) at the magazine's rear bottom corner. It makes the rear of the magazine look thicker at the bottom.
- The magazine is therefore a long, thin horizontal slab rather than a vertical stick, 12 long and 8-9 tall. Its bottom (Y 28.5) is 1.1 above the grip's lowest point (Y 29.6).
- The cartridge (a pale brass round) appears only on the separate magazine item, not on the gun. The gun's magazine is always a closed box.

### Scope
Everything here is centered on the mid-plane and lies on one level axis at about **Y 4.1**, 9.8 above the barrel axis. The scope does not tilt. It is the biggest single object after the barrel assembly.
- **Eyepiece**: a 4 by 4 square bar, 7 long, turned 45 degrees (5.7 across and tall). X 20 to 27, Y 1.2 to 6.9.
- **Rear tube**: a 3 by 3 square bar, 14 long, turned 45 degrees (4.2 across). X 27 to 41, Y 2.0 to 6.2. It is the narrowest part of the scope.
- **Center cube**: an upright 4 by 4 by 4 cube, not turned (X 32 to 36, Y 2.0 to 6.0). It overlaps the tube's middle and is what the turrets mount on.
- **Front tube**: a 4 by 4 square bar, 5 long, turned 45 degrees (5.7 across). X 41 to 46, Y 1.2 to 6.9.
- **Objective bell**: a 5 by 5 square bar, 5 long, turned 45 degrees (7.1 across and tall). X 46 to 51, Y 0.5 to 7.6, Z -3.5 to 3.5. This is the widest part of the entire gun and the front end of the scope.
- **Elevation turret** (on top): a 3 by 2 by 3 block (3 square in plan, 2 tall) turned 45 degrees about the vertical, seen from above as a diamond 4.2 across. X 31.9 to 36.1, Y 0 to 2.0. It sits on the center cube and is the top of the gun.
- **Windage turret** (on the shooter's right side, -Z): a 2 by 3 by 3 block turned 45 degrees about the cross-gun axis, so that in the side view it is a diamond 4.2 across and 4.2 tall, protruding 2 thick (Z -3.8 to -1.8). X 31.9 to 36.1, Y 2.0 to 6.2. It sticks out 1.7 beyond the tube on one side only. There is none on the left.
- **Rings**: two 3 wide, 3 tall, 1 long blocks (Y 4.5 to 7.5) under the tube at X 29 to 30 and X 38 to 39. They are the visible clamps.
- **Mount base**: a 3 wide, 1 tall, 10 long plate (X 29 to 39, Y 7.5 to 8.5), directly on the rail, with the rings rising from its ends.
- Under the scope there is air. The bottom of the eyepiece (Y 6.9) sits 1.6 above the rail (Y 8.5) behind the mount, and the objective (Y 7.6) sits 0.9 above the rail ahead of it. This slot is part of the silhouette; keep it open.
- No lens hood and no sunshade. No scope rail on the sides.

### Shroud
- Two diamond bars end to end, both **4 by 4, turned 45 degrees**, 5.7 across, centered on the barrel's axis (Y 13.85): a rear section 6 long (X 48 to 54) and a front section 8 long (X 54 to 62). Together they are one 14-long fat tube, Y 11.0 to 16.7. Use one bar, with a painted seam at X 54.
- On top sits a **2 by 2 by 14** box (X 48 to 62, Y 10.5 to 12.5), which sticks up 0.5 above the diamond's top corner and gives a flat spine to the shroud.
- The shroud's rear face is flush with the receiver's front face (X 48).

### Floating front rail
- A thin plate **2 wide, 1 tall, 14 long** (X 47 to 61, Y 8.5 to 9.5), exactly level with the main rail's top (Y 8.5), so the rail line is continuous from X 22 to 61.
- It is carried by **four small ribs**, each 2 wide, 1 tall and 1 long, at X 49 to 50, 52 to 53, 55 to 56 and 58 to 59 (Y 9.5 to 10.5), standing on the shroud's flat spine. The gaps between ribs are 2 long. The ribs make the rail look like a perforated bridge.
- At the rail's front end, a **2 wide, 3 tall, 1 thick strut**, tilted 18 degrees so its top leans backward (top at X 60.1 to 61, bottom at X 61 to 62), runs from Y 8.5 down to Y 11.7. It is a small sloped front face that ties the rail's front down to the shroud's front end.
- The rail overlaps the main rail by 1 unit at its rear (X 47 to 48).

### Gas tube
- A very thin bar, **1 by 1, 19 long, turned 45 degrees** (1.4 across), above the barrel: X 62 to 81, Y 10.5 to 11.9, centered on the mid-plane. It is 0.6 above the barrel's top.
- Its rear end butts against the shroud's front face (the flat spine ends at X 62). At the front, a **2 wide, 2 thick, 3 tall brace tilted 38 degrees** (top leaning backward: top at X 79.4 to 81, Y 10.5; bottom at X 81 to 82.9, Y 14.1) runs from the tube's tip down and forward to the front of the mid collar.
- It reads as a thin dark line floating above the barrel from the shroud to the first collar. Seen from the front it is hidden behind the muzzle brake.

### Barrel
- A **2 by 2 square bar, 38 long, turned 45 degrees** (2.8 across): X 54 to 92, Y 12.5 to 15.3, axis at Y 13.9, Z -1.4 to 1.4. The first 8 units are inside the shroud; the exposed bar runs from X 62 to 92.
- Its axis (Y 13.9) matches the shroud's axis (Y 13.85) to within 0.05; keep both on one axis.
- No taper, no fluting, no sight, no rib.

### Mid collar and front collar
- Mid collar: **3 by 3 square bar, 3 long, turned 45 degrees** (4.2 across and tall), X 80 to 83, Y 11.8 to 16.0.
- A **2 wide, 1 tall, 2 long** foot (X 80.5 to 82.5, Y 15.0 to 16.0) under it: a small flat lug that has the same bottom line as the collar's lowest point.
- Front collar: identical, at X 92 to 95. Its foot is X 92.5 to 94.5.
- Between the two collars the bare barrel is 9 long (X 83 to 92). Between the shroud and the mid collar it is 18 long (X 62 to 80).

### Muzzle brake
- **Three baffles**, each a 4 by 4 square bar 1 thick turned 45 degrees (5.7 across), stacked touching at X 95 to 98. They form one 3-thick fat diamond slab, as wide as the shroud. The front collar's rear face meets it at X 95. Paint two dark port squares on its faces (section 5); no cut ports.
- Then a **1-thick gap** (X 98 to 99) in which only two small bits remain: two 1 by 1 diamonds (1.4 across), one at the top (Y 11.8 to 13.2) and one at the bottom (Y 14.6 to 16.0). Seen from the side these are two tiny posts. In the side view you can see through the slot between them and the baffle slab; from the front they are hidden behind the end cap.
- Then the **end cap**: a 3 by 3 square bar 1 thick turned 45 degrees (4.2 across and tall), X 99 to 100, Y 11.8 to 16.0. Its face has a 1-pixel black bore dot in the center. Because the cap is smaller than the slab, you see the slab's front edge as a rim behind it.
- The brake is a **side-vented** one: the 1-unit gaps at the left and right of the posts are the openings.

Shading style:
- **Flat and matte.** No gradients, no scratches, no wear, no noise. Large areas are exactly one color. All detail is painted as sharp-edged bands and blocks of a second color one or two steps lighter or darker.
- **Overall value.** The gun reads as black. The lightest big surfaces are the magazine (#4D4D4D) and the grip stipple (#333333/#3F3F3F). Nothing is lighter than #5E5E5E except the lens tint. There is no specular highlight, no metallic sheen, no bright accent.
- **Receiver sides:** mostly #1E1E1E with a slightly lighter (#333333) horizontal band along the top rear, and a #333333 rectangle (about 16 by 11 pixels at 2 per unit, i.e., 8 units long, 5.5 tall) that reads as a recessed ejection port on the middle-rear. A small dotted panel pattern of #3F3F3F pixels in a checker arrangement sits inside a lower-rear #333333 block (the "magwell" or fire-control panel), plus a #333333 stripe along the bottom.
- **Rail:** the top is a fine alternating pattern of #1E1E1E and #333333 rows, 1 pixel each, running across the rail (a Picatinny slot look); the sides carry a #4D4D4D/#333333 hatched pattern in the lower 7/8 of their length.
- **Barrel:** mostly #1E1E1E with #4D4D4D narrow strips on the top and bottom faces; some #333333 collar marks and two or three small fixture rectangles near the muzzle end.
- **Grip:** a field of #333333 with a diagonal stipple of #3F3F3F pixels in a checker pattern over the large side faces. A flat #333333 top.
- **Magazine:** #4D4D4D body with vertical #333333 ribs about 5 to 6 pixels apart (6 ribs per face at 2 px per unit), plus two #4D4D4D labels. The magazine reads lighter than everything around it.
- **Muzzle brake:** #333333 faces with two #1E1E1E 2-pixel squares on each baffle face for the ports; the end cap has a 1-pixel #000000 center dot.
- **Scope:** the tube, bell and eyepiece are #1E1E1E. The objective end has a 3 by 3 pixel #2B2B47 patch; the eyepiece end has a 2 by 2 pixel #454570 patch. The center cube is #333333 so it stands out against the tubes.

## 7. Motion

- The **model has no moving parts.** The bolt, the magazine, the turrets and the trigger do not move. All motion is whole-gun transforms. The magazine does not swap or disappear on reload.
- **Recoil:** 10-tick animation, flash 5 ticks. First person: a gentle pitch of up to 6 degrees with a 0.25-block push back. Third person: the gun pitches up about 15 degrees around the wrist in roughly the first quarter of the first half of the animation, then settles back.
- **Reload:** 80 ticks (4 s). First person: the gun tips and slides up and aside, hangs near the middle, then drops back. Third person: it tilts about 30 degrees about the cross axis and 40 degrees about the vertical axis at mid-animation, then returns.
- **Optional (not in the original):** the builder may keep the magazine, turrets and trigger as separate named parts so later code can animate them, but the rest pose must match this document.

## 8. What to avoid

No earlier GregGuns attempt of this gun exists yet. These are the mistakes the revolver, SCAR and bolt-action rounds showed in similar builds, and which this gun invites:

- **A short, stubby rifle.** The gun is 100 long and only 30 tall. Check the ratio: 13 : 3.9 : 1.
- **A thick, round barrel running the whole way.** The barrel is a thin 2.8-across bar; the thick sections are the shroud (X 48 to 62) and the brake (X 95 to 98). Don't make a uniform tube.
- **A scope sitting on the receiver with no gap.** The scope tube axis is 9.8 above the barrel axis and its bottom floats 1 to 2 units above the rail. Keep the slot visible. Don't merge the scope into a single big box. Keep the stepped eyepiece, tube, front tube and bell.
- **A scope centered between the turrets.** The windage turret is on the right side only (-Z). The elevation turret is on top.
- **A magazine behind the trigger.** The magazine sits in front of the trigger housing (X 32.5 to 44.5), under the receiver's front half. The grip is behind it, with a closed guard between them.
- **A vertical stick magazine.** It is long (12) and shallow (8 tall visible, 2 wide).
- **A brown or tan gun.** Everything is near-black. The magazine is the only gray piece, and it is a dark gray (#4D4D4D).
- **A cube cheek rest.** The comb is a diamond bar (5.7 across) that peaks above the receiver cap.
- **A solid muzzle block.** The brake has three baffles, a 1-unit side slot with two tiny top and bottom posts, and a smaller end cap.
- **A floating gas tube.** Link it to the shroud's front face and to the diagonal brace at the mid collar.
- **A thin, floating front rail.** The rail needs its four ribs and its sloped end strut, and its top must be level with the main rail's top.
- Do not add things the original lacks: no bipod, no iron sights, no carry handle, no bolt handle, no charging handle, no side rail, no flash hider threads, no sling mounts, no wood.

- [ ] Length : height : width is within about 10 percent of 13 : 3.9 : 1 (100 : 29.6 : 7.6).

## 10. Unsure

- **Scope center cube.** It is 0.2 off the mid-plane toward +Z in the original; I read this as a modeling slip and treat it as centered.
- **Cheek rest gap.** By the numbers the diamond's lowest corner hovers 0.3 above the stock body. I treat that as a gap that should be closed.
- **Gas tube.** Its purpose is my reading: a thin bar above the barrel with a diagonal link to the mid collar. In the original it may be intended as a different kind of handguard rib. The geometry above is what is there.
- **Magazine well.** The pale magazine and dark holder are clear in the texture. How they should read when the magazine is empty is not defined; the gun model draws the same magazine regardless of ammo.
- **Front rail strut angle.** I give 18 degrees with the top leaning back; the sign is from a formula read, not a render. A strut that leans the other way would look odd, so check that the strut meets the shroud's front-top corner.
- **Painted panel details** on the receiver, such as the port and the dotted fire-control panel, are my reading of a small, low-contrast texture; they are optional.
- **First-person framing numbers** come from formulas read, not seen in game. Treat the offsets as approximate.
