# AK-47: clean-room model notes

Design notes for rebuilding the Techguns **Kalaschnikow (AK-47)** look-alike in GregGuns. Written by the "dirty room" analyst from study of the original's model, renderer and texture. They are meant to be complete enough to build from without ever seeing the original. They contain no source, no box lists and no texture coordinates; the builder designs its own UV layout.

Related facts: [ModelAK](../gun-models.md#modelak-12864-family-z-35-boxes-1-part), [AK-47 sheet](../gun-sheets.md#ak-47-ak47), [rendering framework](../rendering-framework.md), [Techguns AK-47 stats](../../02-guns/gun-catalog.md#table-a-identity-and-ammunition).

**Units and axes used in this document.** One unit = one texel at the native 128x64 scale = 1/16 block. Muzzle toward **+X**. **Y points down** (Minecraft model convention), with **Y = 0 at the top line of the dust cover** (the flat top of the receiver cover, which is also the top of the handguard and the gas tube). Negative Y is above that line. **Z = 0 is the gun's mid-plane**; sizes across Z are total widths, centered. **X = 0 is the back face of the buttstock** (the butt plate), so the muzzle is at X = 98. Rounded to the nearest half unit unless it matters. (The original is authored with the muzzle toward -Z; this document flips it so that all the gun notes read the same way.)

## 1. Gestalt

A **long, slim, dark-steel Kalashnikov** with **walnut furniture**, drawn with chunky Minecraft-scale boxes. At a glance you read: a very long, thin silhouette (almost 25 times as long as it is wide); a flat, dark receiver; a **banana magazine that curves forward** under the middle; a straight slab **wooden buttstock** that thickens toward the rear and is stepped on its underside; a wooden handguard around the front of the receiver; a long skinny barrel with a gas tube riding above it; and a tall front-sight post near the muzzle. The grip is a short, raked-back post under the rear of the receiver.

It is a **two-tone gun**: near-black steel and warm brown wood. The camo variant swaps the wood for blue polymer and leaves the steel unchanged.

Defining features in order of visual importance:

1. **The curved magazine**, two stacked slabs that sweep forward and down, deeper than anything else on the gun.
2. **Steel receiver with wooden handguard and stock** at the two ends of the body. Wood at both ends, steel in the middle and out to the muzzle.
3. **The long thin barrel**: 27 units of slender diamond-section bar, reaching well beyond the handguard, with a gas tube above it and a front-sight post near the tip.
4. **The stepped buttstock**: a straight, slab-sided stock whose underside forms a staircase, growing taller toward the butt.
5. **A short raked pistol grip and an open trigger guard** under the rear of the receiver.

## 3. Part by part

All parts are boxes unless noted. Z widths are centered on the mid-plane. "Touches" means faces in contact or overlapping.

### Buttstock
Wood, **4 wide** throughout (the full gun width), built from a main slab plus stepped layers.
- **Main slab:** 26 long (X 0 to 26), 4 tall (Y 4 to 8). It meets the receiver at its rear.
- **Top comb layer:** a 1-tall layer on top of the slab at the **butt end only**, X 0 to 12 (Y 3 to 4). Along X 12 to 24 there is a one-unit **dip** (the slab top is at Y 4), then a tiny 2-long, 1-tall bump at X 24 to 26 (Y 3 to 4) where the stock meets the receiver. The dip reads as a shallow saddle along the top of the stock.
- **Underside staircase:** four 1-tall layers stacked beneath the slab, each shorter than the one above, all flush with the butt plate at X = 0:

| Layer | Y range | Length (from X = 0) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8 to 9 | 21 |
| 2 | 9 to 10 | 15 |
| 3 | 10 to 11 | 10 |
| 4 | 11 to 12 | 7 |

- The butt end is therefore a flat vertical wall **9 tall** (Y 3 to 12) and 4 wide. The stock tapers from 9 units deep at the butt to 4 at the wrist, with a stepped underside and a nearly level top.
- The stock's front end buries into the receiver's rear face, on the same centerline and at the same width as the lower receiver, so the side view reads as one continuous wood slab emerging from the steel.
- No cheek piece, no sling loop, no differently colored butt plate.

### Lower receiver
- A box **28 long, 5 tall, 4 wide** (X 26 to 54, Y 3 to 8). Steel, the full 4-unit gun width. It is the main body.
- Its top is covered by the dust cover (below), so only the 5-tall side wall shows from the side, plus the bottom.
- Touches: the stock at its rear face, the grip and trigger group on its underside, the magazine through its underside, the handguard at its front.

### Dust cover
- A box **23 long, 3 tall, 3 wide** (X 29 to 52, Y 0 to 3), centered over the lower receiver. It is half a unit narrower per side than the lower receiver, so a thin ledge shows on both sides from the top.
- **Rear end:** a 3x3 block, 4 long, rotated about 50 degrees nose-down about the width axis makes a **sloped back** to the cover: the top eases down toward the stock over about 5 units (X 26.5 to 31, from Y 0 down to Y 5). It reads as a chamfered rear end. A tiny 1-unit nub (tilted about 70 degrees, X 27 to 28, Y 1 to 2) sits on the slope; optional.
- **Front end (sight block):** a 4-wide, 3-tall, 7-long block (X 52 to 59, Y 0 to 3) directly in front of the cover and level with it. It is the rear-sight base and the start of the handguard. It is **wider than the cover** by half a unit each side.

### Rear-sight leaf
- A thin bar, **1 wide, 1 tall, 7 long**, lying on the sight block and the front of the dust cover (X 51 to 58), tilted about 8 degrees as a gentle ramp (see section 10 for direction). Its top is at about Y = -1, so it is the **only thing above the top line at the rear**. Plain steel, no notch.

### Handguard
Wood, two pieces that sandwich the barrel base.
- **Lower handguard:** 4 wide, 5 tall, **14 long** (X 54 to 68, Y 3 to 8). It overlaps the front of the lower receiver by a unit or so at X 54 and ends at X 68.
- **Upper handguard:** 3 wide, 3 tall, **9 long** (X 59 to 68, Y 0 to 3), directly on top of the lower one. Its top is at the top line, flush with the sight block, and its back end butts the sight block at X 59.
- The lower piece is half a unit wider per side than the upper, so a small ledge runs along the sides at Y 3. The front faces of both are flat and vertical at X 68.
- The barrel's axis is at Y 5, so the barrel exits the **lower half** of the front face.

### Barrel
- A slender **diamond-section bar**: a 2x2 bar turned 45 degrees about its axis, about **2.8 tall and 2.8 wide** edge-on, **27 long** (X 71 to 98). Axis at about **Y = 5** (the diamond spans Y 3.5 to 6.3).
- It leaves the handguard's front face (X 68) and shows as a clean bar to the muzzle; its rear 3 units are buried in the handguard.
- It carries three chunky **collars** with the same diamond orientation, 4.2 across at their points:
  - **Rear collar:** 3 long (X 68 to 71), snug against the handguard's front face.
  - **Gas block:** 3 long (X 77 to 80), the front mount of the gas tube.
  - **Muzzle collar:** 6 long (X 86 to 92), a thick sleeve under the front sight; 6 units of the thinner bar continue beyond it (X 92 to 98) as the muzzle tip.
- No other taper; no flash hider, no brake, no slots.

### Gas tube and strut
- **Gas tube:** a 2x2 bar turned 45 degrees (diamond, about 2.8 across), **9 long** (X 68 to 77), riding **above** the barrel with its top corner at the top line (Y 0 to 2.8, axis at Y 1.4).
- **Strut:** a short diagonal 2x2 bar, 5 long, tilted about 55 degrees, joining the **front end of the gas tube down to the barrel** at the gas block (X 75.5 to 80, Y 0 to 5). It braces the tube and leaves a small triangular negative space between tube, strut and barrel.

### Front sight
- A **tall thin post**, 1 wide, 1 deep, **6 tall**, standing on the barrel at X 90 to 91, from Y -1 (just over the top line) down to Y 5 (into the barrel).
- A **leaning brace**, also 1x1 and 6 tall, tilted about 21 degrees (top toward the muzzle), directly behind the post: base at about X 87, top meeting the post at X 90, Y -1.4. Together they form a thin slanted "A".
- A **tip:** a 1 wide, 1 tall, 2 long block (X 89 to 91, Y -2 to -1) on top of the post. The very top of the gun is Y = -2.
- From the side the sight is a thin triangle of two bars with a notch of negative space; it must not read as a solid wedge.

### Pistol grip
- A block **3 wide, 4 long, 8 tall**, raked **about 21 degrees back** (the bottom toward the stock): about X 30 to 33 at the top (Y 9.5) to about X 26.5 to 30 at the bottom (Y 18.5). It hangs under the lower receiver's rear, behind the trigger.
- A small **filler** (3 wide, 1 long, 2 tall) at the grip's rear top (X 28 to 29, Y 8 to 10) joins the grip to the receiver's underside so there is no gap.
- **Dark steel, not wood.**

### Trigger group
- **Rear block:** a 3 wide, 5 long, 3 tall box (X 29 to 34, Y 8 to 11) under the lower receiver, in front of the grip. It forms the back of the trigger-guard opening.
- **Guard floor:** a thin bar **2 wide, 1 tall, 7 long** (X 31 to 38, Y 11 to 12), running from over the grip to the front post.
- **Guard front post:** 2 wide, 1 long, 4 tall (X 38 to 39, Y 8 to 12).
- **Magazine-well front lip:** a 3x3x2 block (X 39 to 41, Y 8 to 11) between the guard post and the magazine.
- **Trigger:** a thin blade, 1 wide, 1 long, 3 tall (X 34 to 36, Y 7.5 to 10.7), tilted about 15 degrees (bottom toward the muzzle), hanging inside the opening from the receiver's underside. Steel, slightly lighter than the guard.
- The opening is a rectangle about **4 long (X 34 to 38) by 3 tall (Y 8 to 11)** with the trigger in it, closed at the bottom by the floor bar.

### Magazine
A **banana magazine** of two stacked slabs, each tilted forward, plus thinner inner fillers. All steel, 3 wide (fillers 2 wide, centered).
- **Upper slab:** 3 wide, 5 long, 8 tall, tilted **about 9 degrees** so the bottom is nearer the muzzle. Top at Y 7 (buried in the receiver) at X 41 to 46; bottom at about Y 15.7, X up to 47.3.
- **Lower slab:** 3 wide, 5 long, **9 tall**, tilted **about 20 degrees** forward. Starts at about Y 14 (X 42.3 at the rear edge) and ends at about Y 24, with its front-bottom corner at about X 50. It tilts more than the upper slab, so the magazine curves.
- **Inner fillers:** two 2x2 bars, 8 tall, following the same tilts and set near the front half (X 46 to 49 and 47 to 52), adding a visible step on the front face. Optional.
- Overall the magazine is about **5 long** along X at every height, 3 wide, about 17 tall, with its bottom about 9 units ahead of its top.
- Details: a lighter edge along the front and top of each slab. No ribs, no rounds, no follower.

## 4. Silhouette checks

- **Side (the main view):** a very long, thin horizontal gun. From the rear: a brown stock whose butt end is a tall flat wall and whose underside steps up toward the receiver; a dark receiver with a sloped rear and a thin sight leaf on top; a brown handguard at the front; a dark thin barrel with a thin gas tube above it and a small triangular strut at its front end; a tall thin front-sight post near the muzzle; and below the receiver a **short raked grip**, a **small open trigger guard** with a trigger, and a **magazine curving forward**. Negative space: the notch between grip and magazine; the open trigger guard; the saddle dip along the top of the stock; the wedges between staircase layers under the stock; the triangle under the gas tube and strut; the gap between the sight post and its brace.
- **Top:** a thin spine, 4 wide, the whole length: 4 wide for the stock, receiver and lower handguard, 3 wide over the dust cover and upper handguard (with ledges either side), a slender diamond bar beyond. The collars bulge slightly (4.2). The stock reads as a long brown band at the rear, the handguard as a shorter one near the front.
- **Front:** a tall narrow shape: the muzzle collar's diamond (about 4 wide) near the top, over a slim 4-wide column of receiver and handguard, with the magazine and grip hanging below, and the sight post just above. A dark column about 4 wide and 26 tall.
- **Back:** the butt end is a **flat brown wall, 4 wide and 9 tall**; above it the receiver's sloped rear and the sight leaf poke up a few units; below it the magazine and grip, centered, 3 wide, dark.
- **Underside:** a long thin bar: the stock's staircase as brown bands at the rear; then the grip and the trigger guard; the magazine bottom; the handguard's underside in brown; the barrel's lower diamond edge forward. All centered, 4 wide or less.

## 5. Materials and palette

Two materials, one steel and one wood. The values were sampled from the original's texture regions. The steel is a **very dark neutral gray** in four close levels. The wood is a **warm brown** in two families: **darker brown** on the handguards and **lighter orange-brown** on the stock.

| Part | Material | Base | Highlight | Shadow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lower receiver | dark steel | #232323 | #2A2A2A mid, #333333 on edges | #1E1E1E seams |
| Dust cover, rear slope | dark steel | #2A2A2A | #333333 | #232323 |
| Sight block, sight leaf | dark steel | #232323 | #333333 | |
| Barrel, collars, gas tube, strut | dark steel | #232323 | #333333 along the barrel's top | |
| Front-sight post, brace, tip | dark steel, a touch lighter | #333333 | | |
| Magazine, grip, trigger guard | dark steel | #232323 | #333333 on front edges and magazine faces | #1E1E1E |
| Trigger | steel | #2A2A2A | | |
| Buttstock slab and layers | warm wood | #553116 | #663B1B, #6E401D | #402511 |
| Handguards, lower and upper | dark wood with orange patches | #362113 to #392514 | #6C4225, #7A4F2B patches | |
| Camo 1 stock | blue polymer | #002C6A | #003B83, #00367C | #001744 |
| Camo 1 handguards | blue polymer, near-black base with bright patches | #000F36 to #001541 | #005FC4, #0050A9 patches | |

Shading style:
- **Steel:** flat and very dark (not blued, not shiny), with subtle per-pixel variation between #232323 and #2A2A2A. **Edges and upper faces** carry the #333333 highlight; seams and joins carry #1E1E1E. Large faces are plain, uniformly dark panels. The steel is identical in both texture variants.
- **Wood stock:** mottled brown with scattered lighter orange-brown and darker brown pixels in short horizontal runs, a **horizontal grain** along the stock's length. Each staircase layer shows its own strip of grain. Slightly darker on the bottom faces.
- **Handguards:** darker than the stock: a **near-black brown base** with a scatter of **orange-brown patches** (about 20 percent of the area), like worn, oiled wood. At a distance the handguards read visibly darker than the stock, yet still brown.
- **Camo 1 "Blue":** the same mottling in **blue** (royal blue, black-navy and a few bright cyan-blue pixels) in place of the brown. Steel unchanged.

## 6. In-game presentation

All transforms apply to the model as a whole, in block units after the model's 1/16 scale. The model is first rotated 180 degrees about X (Y-down becomes screen-up). Offsets are in the original's own axes, so treat them as approximate in ours.

| View | Orientation and scale | Offset and notes |
|---|---|---|
| First person | yaw 225 degrees, scale 1.4 | idle offset about (-0.55, -0.35, -0.65). The camera sees the gun from the side and slightly behind, with the muzzle pointing forward and slightly across to the left. |
| First person firing | pitch up to about 5 degrees at peak | plus a backward shift of about 0.15 block along the gun axis at peak. A small, tight kick. |
| First person reloading | pitch up to about 50 degrees, held through the middle of the reload | smooth rise and fall; the gun tips so the magazine is exposed. |

**Muzzle flash point:** the front of the barrel, a flash sprite at 1.5 times scale. Anchor it at the barrel tip (X 98, Y 5, Z 0).

## 7. Motion

- The **model has no moving parts.** The magazine does not drop, the bolt does not cycle and the trigger does not move. All motion is whole-gun transforms.
- **Recoil:** 3-tick fire delay (full auto), flash 5 ticks. First person: a small pitch kick (about 5 degrees) and a 0.15-block pull-back at peak. Third person: the gun pitches up about 15 degrees in the first quarter of the first half of the animation, then settles.
- **Reload:** 45 ticks (2.25 s). First person: the gun tips by up to 50 degrees and returns. Third person: a 30-degree pitch with a 40-degree yaw at mid-reload.
- **Optional (not in the original):** the builder may keep the magazine and trigger as separate named parts so later code can animate them, but the rest pose must match this document.

## 8. What to avoid

- **A short, stubby gun.** The AK is **98 units long and only 4 wide.** The most common failure is cutting the barrel and the stock short. The stock must be 26 long and the barrel 27 long beyond the handguard.
- **A straight, vertical magazine.** It must bow forward in two slabs (about 9 and 20 degrees). A straight box reads as an M4 magazine.
- **A one-color body.** Steel in the middle, wood at both ends, with the handguard visibly darker than the stock. Do not paint the handguard like the receiver.
- **A flat buttstock.** It needs the staircase underside and the 9-tall butt wall.
- **A wooden pistol grip.** The grip is dark steel.
- **A thick barrel shroud.** The barrel is a slim 2.8 diamond; the thick parts are only the three short collars.
- **Modern add-ons.** No rail, scope, optic, underbarrel attachment, flash hider, cheek piece or sling swivel.
- **Shiny or tinted steel.** The steel is flat and very dark.
- **Camo as new geometry.** The blue camo is a texture change only.

## 9. Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Length : height : thickness is within about 10 percent of 24.5 : 6.5 : 1 (98 : 26 : 4).
- [ ] The stock is about 26 long, 4 wide and 9 tall at the butt, with a **staircase underside** of four layers and a flat, vertical butt wall.
- [ ] The stock top has a one-unit dip between its rear comb layer and a small bump at its front end.
- [ ] The receiver is a 28-long, 5-tall, 4-wide steel body with a narrower 3-wide, 23-long dust cover on top and a sloped rear end.
- [ ] A 4-wide, 7-long sight block with a thin sight leaf (top about 1 unit above the top line) sits in front of the dust cover.
- [ ] The handguard is **two pieces**: a 4-wide, 5-tall, 14-long lower piece and a 3-wide, 3-tall, 9-long upper piece, flush with the top line, wood, darker than the stock.
- [ ] The barrel is a slender diamond bar, 27 long beyond the handguard, with three short thicker collars (rear, gas block, muzzle).
- [ ] A diamond **gas tube** about 9 long rides above the barrel, braced by a diagonal strut at the gas block.
- [ ] The front sight is a **tall thin post** (rising 1 unit over the top line, tip at Y = -2) with a leaning brace behind it.
- [ ] The **magazine is a two-slab banana** curving forward (about 9 and 20 degrees), 3 wide, about 17 tall, ending about 24 units below the top line.
- [ ] The grip is **dark steel**, 3 wide, about 8 tall and raked about 21 degrees back.
- [ ] The trigger guard is an open rectangle with a floor bar, a front post and a small tilted trigger inside.
- [ ] Steel is very dark (about #232323 to #333333); the stock is warm brown (about #553116, orange highlights); the handguards are darker brown (about #362113) with orange patches.
- [ ] Camo: **one variant** besides the default (brown wood by default, blue polymer as camo 1), same geometry, identical steel.
- [ ] No part floats; every box overlaps or touches a neighbor.

## 10. Unsure

- **Sight-leaf direction.** The tilt is about 8 degrees; from the renders it reads as a slight ramp. Either direction is acceptable; its top is about 1 unit above the top line.
- **Wood on the dust cover.** A few wood-colored pixels appear in the dust cover's region of the texture. They are shared-region artifacts and should not be reproduced; the dust cover is plain steel.
- **A green front-sight patch.** The texture has an unused patch of green (and a hint of red) near the front-sight area that is not visible on any face in the renders. Optional; a tiny green or white dot on the post's front face is the only plausible reading.
- **Magazine filler bars** are almost completely hidden behind the slabs. Optional.
- **First-person framing numbers** come from formulas read, not seen in game. Treat the offsets as approximate. The muzzle-flash offset is in the renderer's own units, so I give the barrel tip instead.
- **Widths.** The stock, lower receiver and lower handguard are 4 wide; the dust cover, upper handguard, grip and magazine are 3. The 45-degree collars reach about 4.2.

## Round 2 corrections (orchestrator review)

These facts come from comparing the first GregGuns build (B1 round 1) against the original, in the same axes as above (X 0 at the buttplate, Y 0 at the dust-cover top line, Z 0 at the mid-plane). The top and front views matched (1.00 and 0.99 overlap) and the barrel, handguard, stock steps and part layout matched to within 0.3 unit. The side silhouette scored 0.90, and the first three items below explain nearly all of that. The overall size already matches (98 long, 26.2 tall, 4.2 wide).

### Magazine is 2 units too shallow front to back (about half of the side error)

The original magazine is **7 deep** (front to back), not 5. It is built from a main plate and a narrower **front plate** set directly ahead of it, so the front edge of the whole magazine is 2.0 units further forward than the main plate's.
- **Upper segment** (hinged at its top edge, Y 7, tipped 9 degrees so its bottom swings forward): the main plate is 5 long (X 41 to 46), 3 wide (Z -1.5 to 1.5) and 8 tall (Y 7 to 15); the front plate is 2 long (X 46 to 48), **2 wide** (Z -1 to 1) and the same height.
- **Lower segment** (hinged at its top edge, Y 14, tipped 20 degrees): the main plate is 5 long (X 42 to 47), 3 wide and 9 tall (Y 14 to 23); the front plate is 2 long (X 47 to 49), 2 wide and about 8 tall (Y 14.4 to 22.4), tipped the same 20 degrees.
- Measured on the full silhouette from the side, the original magazine is 7.0 to 7.3 deep at every height, with its rear edge following the same curve as round 1's. Round 1 is only 5.0 to 5.1 deep, so the whole front strip, nearly 26 square units, is missing. The front plate is narrower by 0.5 per side, so from the front the magazine shows a 3-wide body with a 2-wide spine ahead of it. Paint both plates the same near-black steel.

### Top cover starts 2 units too far forward

The flat top cover (3 wide, 3 tall, Y 0 to 3) spans **X 29 to 52**, 23 long. Round 1 put it at X 31 to 54. The cover is joined at its rear to a short sloped wedge: the wedge is **4 long** (not 6), tilted 50 degrees, hinged at the cover's rear top edge at X 29, and ends about 3 units lower at X 26.4 where it meets the top of the stock (Y 3). With the cover 2 units too far forward and a 6-long wedge, round 1's top line at X 30 sat 1.3 units below the original's. Slide the cover back 2, shorten the wedge to 4, and the rising line meets the original's within 0.2. The sight block ahead of it (X 52 to 59, Y 0 to 3, 4 wide) is correct and stays.

### Pistol grip hangs 1.2 too short

The grip is a 3-wide, 8-tall, 4-deep block hinged on its **top-front corner** at X 33, Y 11, so its top is 3 units below the receiver's underside (Y 8), with the filler block above it. It leans 21.3 degrees so its bottom swings 2.9 rearward. Its lowest point is the front-bottom corner at about X 30.1, Y 18.5, and its rear-bottom corner sits at about X 26.4, Y 17.0. Round 1 hinged it on its top **center** at Y 9, 2 units too high, so its lowest corner stopped at Y 17.2, which is 1.3 short. Hinge it at the top-front corner, at Y 11, keeping the 21-degree lean.

### Small top furniture (each about 0.7 unit)

- The rear sight leaf (7 long, 1 tall, X 51 to 58) is **embedded in the cover's top** (Y 0 to 1), tilted 8 degrees, so it rises above the cover line by 1.0 at most at its high end and is flush at its low end. Round 1 floats a flat leaf 0.9 above the cover along its whole length.
- The front-sight A-frame's brace leans 21 degrees with its top end rearward, so at X 88 the original's top edge sits about 0.7 higher than round 1's.

### Paint (from the round 1 sheet)

- Steel: the original is a flat **#252525** (54 percent of the texture) with #333333 highlights (9 percent); nothing darker than #1F1F1F except the sight hole. Round 1 put about 10 percent of its texels at #1D1D1D, which reads as dirty patches. Lift those to #252525.
- Wood: base **#583318** (8 percent) and mid **#653A1A** (7 percent), with highlights **#784828** and shadow flecks **#452914** and **#372313**. The original's wood is mottled; every wood face has about 6 to 10 scattered highlight texels and a similar number of shadow flecks. Round 1's whole texture averaged 4 to 5 levels darker (#35281F against #392D22) and its grain was too regular.
