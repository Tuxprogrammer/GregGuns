# 04: Projectiles and FX

## Overview


Techguns 1.7.10 (v0.1.2 alpha). This area covers everything a gun puts into the world after the trigger is pulled:

- projectile and beam entities
- their renderers
- explosions
- the client particle system and its data files
- muzzle-flash and scope overlay sprites
- special death animations (gore, bio-dissolve, laser-disintegrate)
- the `textures/fx/` art and `sounds/effects/` audio

Gun stats and firing logic are in [`02-guns`](../02-guns/index.md). Gun models and first-person rendering are in [`03-gun-visuals-audio`](../03-gun-visuals-audio/index.md). The damage pipeline ([`DamageSystem`](../02-guns/index.md#coverage), [`TGDamageSource`](../02-guns/damage-system.md#2-tgdamagesource-extends-vanilla-entitydamagesource), armor penetration, friendly fire) is in [`01-core`](../01-core/index.md).

## Key facts

- 26 projectile/FX entities are registered (mod-local IDs 1–26). [`AlienBlasterProjectile`](projectiles.md#1-registration) is fired by a live gun but is **not** registered. [`RailgunProjectile`](projectiles.md#316-railgunprojectile-never-spawned), [`BlasterProjectile`](projectiles.md#318-blasterprojectile-deprecated), [`PiercingProjectile`](projectiles.md#317-piercingprojectile-deprecated-unregistered) and [`FlashBangProjectile`](projectiles.md#1-registration) are also unregistered, but nothing reachable spawns them.
- Projectiles fly in straight lines at `speed` blocks/tick with 0.99 drag (0.8 in water) and optional constant gravity. They ray-trace blocks on both sides and entities on the server only, and pass through non-solid blocks such as grass.
- Bullets ignore hurt-resistance. Knockback comes from a separate 0.01-damage "dummy" hit.
- Damage falls off linearly with *distance traveled* between `dmgDropStart` and `dmgDropEnd`.
- Explosions: flat damage inside r1 (0.5 × radius) falling to 0 at r2 (1.5 × radius). Blocks break per vanilla ray-casting when the shooter's safe mode is off. The shooter is immune to their own blast.
- All visuals are client-side and driven by FX packets. Named effects are defined in two text data files.

## Coverage

- **Source files read:** all 30 classes in `java/techguns/entities/projectiles/` (every one has behavior in projectiles.md sections 2 and 3 and a row in the section 4.5 summary; the four thinnest, `GenericProjectile`, `SimpleProjectile`, [`InvisibleProjectile`](projectiles.md#43-invisibleprojectile) and `BlasterProjectile`, have full sheets in sections 4.1 to 4.4); the projectile and beam renderers and `ScreenEffect` in `client/renderer/`; `util/ProjectileExplosion.java` and `TGExplosionDamageSource`; the whole `client/particle/` package and its two data files; the death-effect classes (`DeathEffect`, `GoreData`, `EntityDeathUtils`, the corpse renderers under `client/renderer/death/`); and the out-of-scope callers listed in Scope notes (packets, event handler, proxy switch). The NPC renderers are deliberately left to [`10-entities-npcs`](../10-entities-npcs/index.md).
- **Textures:** 49 of 49 `textures/fx/*.png` and 7 of 7 projectile textures in `textures/entity/` (`bullet`, `handgunBullet`, `bioblob`, `BlueBeam`, `Laser`, `launchergrenade`, `gore`) have size, measured average and dominant colors, a palette in words and the frame grid as pixel rectangles (textures.md, "Measured palette and frame rectangles"). Textures referenced in code but absent are listed under "Missing textures".
- **Sounds:** 26 of 26 `sounds/effects/*.ogg` have duration, sample rate, channels and category (sounds.md, "Measured audio facts"); all 11 distinct events plus the vanilla sounds used by this area's code have trigger, volume and pitch.
- **Lang:** all 17 `death.attack.*` keys, the one projectile entity name and the 9 `techguns.TGDamageType.*` keys are listed in [lang.md](lang.md), with the missing keys (PFX-31).
- **Corrections made in this pass:** the sounds.md "character" table guessed ~0.5 s for the bullet impacts and ~1 to 2 s for the explosions; the measured values are 0.98 s and 1.21 to 2.85 s, and the cells now say so. The previous wording of the death-message fallback for missing `.item` keys is corrected in lang.md.

### Scope notes

- Projectile entity registrations live in [`java/techguns/Techguns.java`](../01-core/lifecycle-and-registration.md#lifecycle-overview) (lines 487–514), not in [`TGEntities.java`](../10-entities-npcs/mob-catalog.md#registration-javatechgunstgentitiesjava). `TGEntities.java` was read in full and contains only NPC registrations and spawn setup ([`10-entities-npcs`](../10-entities-npcs/index.md)).
- The death-type trigger chain spans files outside the listed scope ([`client/DeathEffect.java`](../01-core/client-effects-audio.md#deatheffectcreatedeatheffectentity-type-mx-my-mz), `client/GoreData.java`, [`packets/PacketEntityDeathType.java`](../01-core/networking.md#6--packetentitydeathtype-sc), `packets/PacketSpawnParticle*.java`, [`events/TechgunsEventhandler.java`](../01-core/events.md#techgunseventhandler-forge-bus), the legacy particle switch in [`client/ClientProxy.java`](../01-core/lifecycle-and-registration.md#proxy-api-surface-commonproxy-overridden-by-clientproxy)). They were read and their behavior is documented here because it is the only way to specify the effects. Ownership of those files remains with [`01-core`](../01-core/index.md).
- NPC/mob renderers in `client/renderer/entity/` ([`RenderAlienBug`](../10-entities-npcs/rendering-and-models.md#4-renderalienbug-extends-renderliving), [`RenderGenericNPC`](../10-entities-npcs/rendering-and-models.md#2-rendergenericnpc-extends-vanilla-renderbiped), [`RenderHelicopter`](../10-entities-npcs/rendering-and-models.md#5-renderhelicopter-extends-rendererlivingentity), [`RenderSimpleTurret`](../10-entities-npcs/rendering-and-models.md#6-rendersimpleturret-extends-plain-render-for-npcturret), [`RenderSuperMutant`](../10-entities-npcs/rendering-and-models.md#3-rendersupermutant-extends-rendergenericnpc)) are left to [`10-entities-npcs`](../10-entities-npcs/index.md).

## Known limits

- Audio and images were not opened by a person; all sound descriptions are inferred from names and use. Colors, sizes, durations and rates are machine-measured (ASSET-FACTS.md).
- The `techscope.png` window bounds (about 1260 x 910 px, centered) were estimated from a scaled view with an error of roughly 10 px.
- Frame cells of `fireblast.png` (102.4 px) and `dustclouds02.png` (85.33 px) are not integers; the grid positions given are the ones the sampling code produces.
- Behavior that runs inside the integrated server in single player ([PFX-04](#suspected-bugs)) was reasoned from the code, not run.
- Projectile "fired by" lists name the gun classes and registry names; full per-gun stat blocks are in [`02-guns`](../02-guns/index.md) and were not restated.
- Visual descriptions of FX sprite sheets were made from the images by eye in the first pass; where they differ from the measured numbers the numbers win.
