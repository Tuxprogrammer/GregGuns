# 0014 · End goal: a working beta, a handoff, then archive

- Status: Accepted (owner, 2026-10-20)
- Gate: none (changes the roadmap's final milestone)
- Date: 2026-10-20

## Context
The roadmap ended with M9, "Pack integration and release". That meant a balance pass, a drafted PR to the GTNH modpack, an outreach package for the pack maintainers and a quest-line handoff, all aimed at getting GregGuns accepted into GTNH.

The [inclusion assessment](../research/gtnh-inclusion-assessment.md) reviewed GTNH's AI policy against how GregGuns is made. GregGuns is built by AI agents under one human owner. Its code, docs and media are AI-generated or generated by AI-written code, and its history carries `Co-Authored-By` tags. The policy bans AI-generated media and those tags, and it judges submissions by standards with no test and no procedure. Submitting GregGuns as it stands would most likely be closed. The assessment also records the owner's critique of the policy.

## Decision
The owner will not submit GregGuns to GTNH and will not personally rework it to fit the policy, as a matter of principle. The project's end goal is now:

1. **A fully working beta.** The beta review gate stays as it is: M0–M8 audited, a clean client pass including a live run in the real GTNH 2.9 pack, and `0.3.0-beta` published.
2. **A handoff package** in the repo for whoever adopts the mod. It covers:
   - the state of every area;
   - how to build and test, including the real-pack test harness;
   - exactly what stands between the mod and GTNH's AI policy;
   - a list of the open work.
3. **A solicitation message,** which the owner posts. It announces that the mod exists, is nearly fully integrated with the pack, and is free under MIT for any developer willing to sponsor it: review it, bring it into compliance, and carry it into the pack. It is open about how the mod was made. It also invites the pack team to consider an exception.
4. **Archive the GitHub repository** once the above is published, on the owner's go-ahead.

## Consequences
- **M9 is replaced** by "Handoff and archive". The GTNH PR draft, the pack-maintainer outreach package and the quest-line handoff are dropped. Nobody from this project opens a PR to GTNH or contacts its maintainers.
- **Work moves to the adopter list in the handoff guide:** the balance pass, GuideNH pages, the OpenModularTurrets investigation, aim down sights, translation readiness and the multiplayer soak. [PACK-CHANGES.md](../gregguns-spec/PACK-CHANGES.md) stays, as the adopter's checklist of pack-side changes.
- **The handoff guide states the provenance plainly.** That gives an adopter an honest starting point for whatever review or rework the pack requires.

## Alternatives considered
- **Submit anyway and argue the policy.** Rejected: the assessment expects it to be closed, and it could get the author blocked from contributing.
- **The owner reworks the media and history to comply.** Rejected by the owner on principle.
- **Keep developing toward 1.0 without the pack.** Rejected: the mod's purpose is the pack, and without a sponsor there it has no audience.
