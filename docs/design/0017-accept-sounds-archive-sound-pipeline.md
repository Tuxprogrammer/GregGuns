# 0017 · Accept the sounds as they are; archive the sound pipeline

- Status: Accepted (owner, 2026-10-25)
- Gate: D6 (amends [0006](0006-art-direction.md))
- Date: 2026-10-25

## Context
GregGuns' sounds are procedurally generated by `tools/asset-gen/gen_sounds.py` and were tuned against the originals they replace over several review rounds, ending with a clean-room blueprint judge. That work was stopped for the MVP on 2026-10-19. The remaining differences are listed in SOUND-DIFFERENCES.md (kept locally since 2026-10-26; see the amendment below), and the roadmap's "Later" section kept an item to revisit the generators.

The end goal is a handoff to an adopter ([0014](0014-end-goal-beta-handoff-archive.md), [0015](0015-m9-self-contained-polish.md)). An adopter who brings the mod into a pack will want its own assets, made by artists it commissions, so more tuning of the generated sounds has little value.

## Decision
1. **The current sounds are final for GregGuns.** No more review rounds, gate runs or generator revisions.
2. **The sound review pipeline is archived.** The gate, the embedding check, the A/B review tooling and SOUND-DIFFERENCES stay in the repository as a record, but are not maintained.
3. The roadmap's "revisit the sound generators" item is closed, not deferred.
4. **Replacing generated assets with commissioned ones** goes on the adopter list.

## Consequences
- Sound gaps in the exit-gate audits (for example M2 G4, the files still at `tweak`) are closed by this record.
- `gen_sounds.py` still reproduces the shipped `.ogg` files from fixed seeds, so the assets stay rebuildable.
- The handoff guide tells the adopter that every texture, model and sound is a generated placeholder it may replace.

## Alternatives considered
- **Keep the "Later" item open.** Rejected: nobody will work on it before the handoff, and an open item would suggest otherwise.
- **Finish the clean-room loop first.** Rejected: the owner has heard and approved the current sounds, and an adopter would replace them anyway.

## Amendment, 2026-10-26
The owner moved the review records out of the repository. `SOUND-DIFFERENCES.md`, the sound gate, loudness and A/B reports, the sound contact sheet and the A/B review log (`tools/asset-gen/sound-ab-review.jsonl`) are now git-ignored and kept locally. The generators, their inputs and the review tooling stay in the repository.
