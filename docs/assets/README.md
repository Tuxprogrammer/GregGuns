# Assets

## Sounds

The sounds are accepted as they are, and the sound review pipeline is archived ([0017](../design/0017-accept-sounds-archive-sound-pipeline.md)). Its review records (`SOUND-DIFFERENCES.md`, the gate and A/B reports) are kept locally, not in the repository.

## Branding and infographics

All generated by `python tools/asset-gen/branding.py` (SVG sources, then PNG exports; `--svg-only` skips the rasterizer). Original artwork, not derived from Techguns. Credits: [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

**branding/**
- `logo.svg`, `logo_128.png` (mcmod.info `logoFile`), `logo_512.png`: steel hex casing, rifle silhouette, GT blue cable and orange bullet.
- `logo_pixel.svg`, `logo_pixel_32.png`, `_128`, `_256`: pixel-crisp 32 x 32 variant for small sizes.
- `banner.svg` / `banner.png`: 1280 x 320, README header.
- `social_preview.svg` / `social_preview.png`: 1280 x 640, GitHub social preview.

**infographics/** (player guides, built by `tools/asset-gen/infographics/build.py`; see its README)
- `hero`: README banner. `getting-started`: the first six steps and default keys.
- `arsenal-steam-to-hv`, `arsenal-ev-to-zpm`: every weapon, rendered, with role and ammo.
- `ammo-and-magazines`, `armor-and-gear`, `machines`, `enemies`, `questbook`: one topic each.
- `recipes-steam-and-lv`: the Steam and LV crafting chains, read from the mod's recipe tables.
- `icbm-warheads`: the ICBM warheads from Tier 1 to nuclear, blast types, fill and radius, fallout, demolition use and machines (built by `pages_icbm.py`). Shows HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod warhead models (LGPL-3.0), credited in its footer.
- `icbm-first-missile`: from a missile chip to the first Tier 1 or 2 launch (built by `pages_icbm_missile.py`). Shows Galacticraft's launch pad and rocket part icons (MIT) and the NTM missile models (LGPL-3.0), credited in its footer.
- `icbm-missile-silo`: the Missile Silo as found in the world, the Tier 3 body and the silo's bonuses (built by `pages_icbm_silo.py`). It paints in two NewHorizonsCoreMod icons, so the sheet is GPL-3.0; it also shows Galacticraft art (MIT), NTM models (LGPL-3.0) and tinted Minecraft grass and leaves.
- `multiblock-ammunition-works`, `multiblock-armory-gantry`, `multiblock-rampart`: build guides (the Works guide shows the optional filling bench; both Works and Gantry show the required interior lamp).
- They are written for players and stand alone: no links to the repository, specs or IDs. They show GregTech 5
  Unofficial textures (LGPL-3.0) and Natura's workbench texture (CC BY 3.0) beside our own.
