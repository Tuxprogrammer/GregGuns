# GregGuns 0.2.0-alpha (pre-release)

> [!WARNING]
> **This is still an early alpha of a mod that is very much a work in progress.** Expect bugs, crashes, missing content, and balance that is nowhere near final. **Back up your world before trying it**, and don't put it on a server or a save you care about.

GregGuns is a from-scratch, Techguns-inspired rewrite for **GT New Horizons** on Minecraft 1.7.10. It isn't a port and isn't affiliated with Techguns or its author.

## Read this first

- **Targets GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2.** This build has been tested headless on a dedicated server, with a client boot. Single-player play of the new content is still light.
- **Content is switched off by default.** After the first launch, edit `config/gregguns.cfg`, enable the modules you want, then restart:
  ```
  modules {
      B:guns=true
      B:armor=true
      B:machines=true
      B:utility=true
      B:npcs=false
  }
  ```
  Leave `npcs` off unless you want to see unfinished work. It is mid-development (see below).
- **Nothing is stable between alphas.** Items, recipes, machine IDs and config keys can change or disappear in later builds.
- **The energy weapons have barely been seen in game.** They pass automated tests, but expect visual issues in how they're held, their effects and their beams.
- **Balance hasn't started.** Damage, charge sizes, recipe costs and tier gates are first drafts.

## What's new since 0.1.0

- **The full ballistic arsenal (Steam to HV).** All 22 guns are in, each with a new 3D model built clean-room from written notes rather than copied. The new guns are the Thompson, Mac-10, M4, Infiltrator, AK-47, AUG, SCAR, Vector, AS50, LMG, Combat Shotgun, Rocket Launcher, Grenade Launcher, Flamethrower, Golden Revolver and the akimbo pairs.
- **Guns are now the right size in your hands.** Every gun's first- and third-person size and position now matches its intended look. The Hand Cannon in particular was far too small before.
- **Energy and exotic weapons (EV to ZPM).**
  - **Ballistic EV:** PDW, Pulse Rifle and Minigun.
  - **Energy rifles:** Laser Rifle, Tesla Rifle with chain lightning, and Sonic Rifle.
  - **IV weapons:** Blaster Rifle, De-Atomizer (plus Akimbo) and Scatter Laser.
  - **LuV and ZPM weapons:** Nether Blaster, Alien Blaster, Grim Reaper, Nuclear Death Ray and Nuke Launcher.
  - **Tool and fluid guns:** Bio Gun, Power Hammer (plus Advanced) and Chainsaw.
  - **How they're powered:** energy guns are real electric items that charge in GregTech batteries and chargers, and the HUD shows their charge. They have no reload.
- **New ammo:** Advanced Bullets, Advanced Magazine, Minigun Ammo Drum, Bio Tank, Nether Charge and Tactical Nuke Rocket. The last two are made on GregTech's Assembly Line.
- **Effects:** a particle and effects system with a catalog of muzzle flashes, beams, trails and explosions, including nuke-scale blasts. Large blasts spread their block breaking across several ticks so the server doesn't stall.
- **Utility and defense:** turrets, explosive charges, the repair and camo benches, and the Rampart multiblock.
- **Sounds:** the gun and effect sounds are reworked and reviewed.
- **Player guide:** infographics in the README cover the arsenal, ammo, armor, machines and enemies.

## Not in it yet

- **NPCs and mobs:** in progress. The zombie soldiers are in, and the other factions, bosses and the attack helicopter are landing over the next builds. Spawn eggs work. Natural spawning and camps are still being finished.
- **Structures and worldgen:** next on the roadmap.
- **The questbook:** lives in the GTNH pack and comes at release.

See [docs/ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/blob/master/docs/ROADMAP.md).

## Known issues

- **Mob textures:** with `npcs` enabled, some mobs may show a missing texture. A fix is landing.
- **Visual details:** some glowing parts of energy weapons are painted, not lit.
- **Recipes:** a few recipes differ from the reference design, for example the Blaster Rifle's missing lens. The differences are documented in the spec.

## Feedback

Bug reports are very welcome on [Issues](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/issues). Please include your GTNH version, Java version, the crash report or `fml-client-latest.log`, and what you were doing at the time.
