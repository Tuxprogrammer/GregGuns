# GregGuns 0.3.2-beta (pre-release)

> [!WARNING]
> **This is a beta.** Balance is still being tuned, and the shader fixes in this build have not been confirmed in a real shader session yet (see Known issues). Back up your world first.

Fixes and balance from the first week of beta play, plus one new weapon.

## Headline

- **Damage balance.** High-end weapons now hit much harder. See [design record 0016](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/blob/master/docs/design/0016-damage-balance-pass.md) for every gun, old and new.
- **Nuke Launcher and Nuclear Death Ray are annihilators.** The Nuke kills anything that is not a boss close to the blast.
- **Golden Gun.** A new LuV pistol with a single golden round that kills in one hit.
- **Minigun spin-up.** The Minigun must reach full barrel speed before it fires, and spins down after you let go.
- **Questbook layout redone,** with hidden tier-skip quests.
- **Shader-pack fixes** for effects that made water, clouds and your gun vanish.

## Upgrading

Drop-in from 0.3.1-beta. Replace the jar on the server and on every client. No config or world changes are needed. Server owners: enemy NPCs keep their old damage, so the world is not harder.

## New

- **Golden Gun** (LuV). The 9mm Pistol in polished gold with a one-round magazine and a slow reload. Anything that is not a boss dies from one hit, armor or not. Bosses lose half their max health. Server damage settings (PvP) still apply. It is made in the Assembler from a 9mm Pistol, 8 gold plates, 2 LuV circuits and an HSS-G plate, and it fires **Golden Rounds** (a 9mm bullet, 2 gold foil and a gold plate).
- **Minigun spin-up.** Hold the trigger for 0.75 seconds (15 ticks) to reach full speed before the first shot. The barrels spin down over 1.5 seconds (30 ticks) after you let go, and pressing again resumes from the current speed. A tap fires nothing. The server enforces the delay. Switching items or reloading stops the barrels.
- **Questbook tier-skip quests.** Seven hidden quests, one per tier from LV up, detect that tier's gate plate in your inventory without taking it. They complete the GregGuns tier heads up to that tier, the same way the pack's own tier-skip quests work.

## Changed

- **Damage balance (all weapons).** Higher tiers now clearly out-damage lower ones. For example the Deatomizer Pistol goes 16 to 32, the Blaster Rifle 10 to 24, the Alien Blaster 16 to 44, the Grim Reaper 50 to 90 and the Scatter Laser 6 to 12 per beam. The tiers are checked against Draconic Evolution and EnderIO weapons. **Enemy NPCs and the attack helicopter keep their old damage.** Your Rampart turret uses the new values.
- **Nuke Launcher.** Inside 6 blocks of the blast, anything that is not a boss dies, through any armor and any brief invulnerability. This includes players, unless server damage settings block it. Bosses lose half their max health. Farther out, damage falls off from 400 to 0 at 15 blocks. A direct hit now explodes on the target.
- **Nuclear Death Ray.** 30 damage per tick, and armor no longer protects against it.
- **HEV suit in water.** Its water bonus no longer changes your movement speed (that had stretched the field of view to about 170 degrees). It is now a real swim boost of about twice vanilla swim speed, and the armor's speed bonus can never push the field of view past vanilla sprinting with Speed II.
- **Laser rifles' muzzle flash.** The Scatter Laser, Laser Rifle and Blaster Rifle now use the laser flare at its intended size, not the larger gunpowder burst.
- **Questbook layout.** Normal-size icons on a compact grid, tiers running left to right, short single lines, and no more thick lines (nothing is marked "main" anymore). The tier heads now chain, so each tier needs the one before it. Extra prerequisites are drawn only when you hover or hold Shift. Quest IDs, text and rewards are unchanged, so progress carries over.

## Fixes

- **Shader packs: water, clouds and your gun vanished** for a few ticks after firing an energy weapon. Effects that start at your eye (laser start flares, muzzle effects, trails) now start at the muzzle, our particles no longer block what is drawn behind them, and nothing is drawn at the camera. This also shrinks the screen-filling white starburst. Unconfirmed in a real shader session; see Known issues.
- **Model flicker (z-fighting)** where model parts overlap, for example at the top of the De-Atomizer's grip, fixed on every gun model. A new test finds these automatically.
- **Questbook install** no longer makes BetterQuesting back up its database on the next start.
- Several beam and bullet renderers left graphics state behind that broke shader packs. These now restore it.

## Server owner notes

- `questbook.install` in `config/gregguns.cfg` is unchanged. In the GTNH pack the GregGuns tab goes back to the last position on every start, because the pack reloads its default quests each boot. Player progress is kept.
- The new layout and tier-skip quests apply on the next start with `questbook.install=true`. Old quest positions are replaced; nobody's progress is lost.

## Known issues

- **Shader fixes are not confirmed live.** They pass all automated tests but have not been checked in a real session with Complementary Unbound and Euphoria Patches. Please report if water or clouds still flicker after firing.
- **Minigun:** other players' barrels start spinning at their first shot, not when they press the trigger. Enemy NPCs with Miniguns fire without the spin-up. There is no spin-up sound yet.
- The held item's name pops up over the hotbar after each shot (cosmetic).
- Everything listed for [0.3.0-beta](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/releases/tag/0.3.0-beta) still applies.
