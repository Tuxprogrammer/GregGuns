## Changed

- **GregGuns Coins go in the Vending Machine's coin slot.** The GregGuns Coin is now one of the machine's wallet coins, next to the pack's own. Put coins in the coin slot (or right-click the machine holding them), and GregGuns trades take their price from your wallet, in any mix of coin values. Ejecting the balance gives GregGuns Coins back, largest first. The coin's tooltip says where it goes.
