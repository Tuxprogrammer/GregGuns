## Changed

- Structure spawners are now vanilla monster spawners. The Big Bunker, the Alien Bug Nest and the ICBM silo guard posts place Minecraft's own spawner with the GregGuns mobs inside; the Zombie Hole, Bug Hole, Silo Guard Post and Silo Elite Post blocks are gone, and any of them in a world from an earlier build turn to air. Guards still start at the complex's danger with matching gear, and still spawn in lit rooms.
