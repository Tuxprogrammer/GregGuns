# GregGuns 0.4.0-alpha (pre-release)

> [!WARNING]
> **This is an alpha of the new ICBM module, and the module is off by default.** It has been tested on a dedicated test server and in the GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2 server pack, but not yet in a long playthrough. Back up your world before you switch it on, and try it in a copy, not your main save.

GregGuns 0.4.0 adds an optional ICBM module: warheads, missiles that fly from Galacticraft launch pads and missile silos, radars and air defense, endgame warheads, and abandoned silo complexes on every planet, all built on GregTech tiers. Everything from 0.3.3-beta is still here and unchanged with the module off.

## Read this first

- **Targets GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2.** The module needs Galacticraft, which the pack ships. OpenComputers is optional.
- **The ICBM module is off unless you switch it on.** Set it in `config/gregguns.cfg` and restart the server:
  ```
  modules {
      B:icbm=true
  }
  ```
  The server's setting applies to everyone who joins. With the module off, its content does nothing and stays out of NEI.
- **Nothing in the ICBM module is stable yet.** Recipes, numbers and config keys can change before the beta.

## Upgrading

Drop-in from 0.3.3-beta. Replace the jar on the server and on every client. The config file gains an `icbm` section, new `explosions.fallout*` keys and `compat.enderioSpawnerBlacklist`, all with safe defaults. Worlds keep working with the module off. The Zombie Hole, Bug Hole, Silo Guard Post and Silo Elite Post blocks are gone; any of them in an existing world turn to air (see Changed).

## New: the ICBM module

- **Warheads, T1 (HV) to T5 (UHV).** Each one is an Assembler recipe at its casing tier, from real GTNH explosives: Condensed, Fragmentation, Incendiary, Chemical, Thermobaric and more at T1 and T2; Exothermic, Endothermic, Nuclear, EMP, Ender and Anti-Gravitational at T3; Antimatter (UV) and Red Matter (UHV). Placed alone, a warhead is a demolition charge with a fuse that grows with its blast. Nuclear, antimatter and red matter kill everything living in their kill zone, bosses included.
- **Missiles, T1 to T3.** Build a body on Galacticraft's NASA Workbench or by Assembly Line behind a research scan, stand it on a Galacticraft launch pad, seat a warhead on its nose, fuel it with Galacticraft rocket fuel, and fire it from the **Launch Control** by coordinates, data stick, redstone or a GT machine controller cover. Missiles fly a real arc, hold at most two chunks, and never harm the pad. Ranges: 1,000 blocks at T1, 3,000 at T2, 8,000 at T3.
- **Missile Silo.** A GT multiblock that takes body, warhead and fuel through hatches, opens its doors, counts down and launches with no hand on the missile. T3 missiles launch only from a silo.
- **Detection and defense.** HV and EV radars (small multiblocks with a spinning dish) and the IV Radar Tower; the Air-Defense Battery and its SAMs; the EMP Tower, which drains stored EU and jams multiblocks nearby. Handheld: Radar Gun, Remote Detonator, Defuse Kit. Every machine talks redstone, data sticks and, with OpenComputers, `gg_*` components.
- **Golden Dome** (with OpenComputers): a floppy with `launch`, `radarwatch`, `autodefense` and `emp` programs.
- **Fallout.** Nuclear strikes leave Irradiated Earth, and the strongest ones leave Trinitite and Fallout Goo, which GT machines process into a small share of the core's fuel. The **Antidote** clears radiation sickness and gas effects.
- **Launcher warheads.** Craft a warhead charge onto the Rocket Launcher, Grim Reaper, Grenade Launcher or Nuke Launcher, and its shots carry that blast type.
- **Abandoned silo complexes** on the Overworld and on every Galacticraft, GalaxySpace and Amun-Ra planet with solid ground (T Ceti E, an ocean world, has none): an underground base about 240 by 240 blocks across, on one flat level 24 blocks under the ground.
  - **Layout.** A 51 by 40 assembly hub, three or four 20-wide major halls leaving it, and minor hallways off the halls. 35 to 40 rooms branch off the halls and off each other, so not every room opens onto the hub. One hall is the silo gallery, with Launch Control at its far end behind a window that overlooks the silos.
  - **Silos.** 4 to 10 real silos line the gallery in facing pairs, each missing a part for you to repair. Radar stations, a missile assembly floor with a NASA Workbench, a warhead magazine, generator hall, armory and more fill the rooms.
  - **Look.** Rooms are built from the pack's decorative blocks (Chisel, Ztones and GregTech), and the floor, walls and ceiling of every room are three different blocks. Lamps sit in a grid in the ceiling, with light tiles in the floor of tall rooms, so a lit complex is lit end to end.
  - **Furniture.** With BiblioCraft installed, the small rooms are furnished: cafeteria tables and chairs, guard-post lockers (from GregTech) and desks, bunks, bookcases and server racks. Without it, plain blocks stand in. The big bays stay open.
  - **Guards and loot.** Guards spawn from vanilla monster spawners, armed to match the complex's danger. The loot ceiling rises with the planet's rocket tier, from EV on the Overworld and the Moon to UMV, and is rolled per complex on Ross128.
- **Guides and quests.** Seven new player guide sheets (Warheads, Heavy Warheads, Your First Missile, Missile Silo, Detection and Defense, Fallout, Abandoned Silos) and ICBM quests in the GregGuns questbook tab.

## Changed (all players)

- **Connected textures.** GregGuns multiblock casings (Ammunition Works, Armory Gantry, Bastion Plating) join into one surface through the pack's connected-texture support, and the Gantry Beam's hazard stripe runs seamlessly.
- **Nuke Launcher fallout.** With `explosions.blockDamage` on, a Nuke Launcher blast leaves Irradiated Earth around its crater, which decays back over time. `explosions.fallout` and the `explosions.fallout*` keys control it.
- **Structure spawners are vanilla monster spawners.** The Big Bunker, the Alien Bug Nest and the silo complex guard posts place Minecraft's own spawner with the GregGuns mobs inside. The Zombie Hole, Bug Hole, Silo Guard Post and Silo Elite Post blocks no longer exist, and any of them in an old world turn to air; the structures that placed them now place vanilla spawners the next time they generate. Guards still start at the structure's danger with matching gear, and still spawn in lit rooms. Breaking a spawner ends it and drops nothing.
- **NPCs.** Bullets and hitscan shots pass through NPCs of the shooter's own faction, so crowded guards no longer wound each other. Explosions still hurt everyone.

## Server owner notes

- **`compat.enderioSpawnerBlacklist`** (default on): puts every GregGuns mob on EnderIO's Powered Spawner blacklist. GregGuns mobs cannot be farmed in a Powered Spawner, and a broken GregGuns spawner drops no Broken Spawner and bursts no extra guards. Turn it off to treat them like any other mob.
- **Spawner hardness** follows GregTech's harder mob spawners: slow to mine by hand, with guards firing. A warhead destroys a guard post outright.
- Every new key is in `docs/config.md`. The `icbm.*` keys cover blast budgets and radii, per-tier terrain damage, missile fuel and range factors, the silo bonus, EMP limits, and the silo complex switches (`icbm.siloComplexEnabled`, `icbm.siloComplexDimensions`, `icbm.siloComplexChancePercent`, `icbm.siloComplexActivityWeights`, `icbm.siloComplexRoss128Weights`).
- ICBM blasts follow their own `icbm.terrainDamageT1` to `T4` switches (T4 covers antimatter and red matter), not `explosions.blockDamage`. Claims and protection mods are always respected.
- At most 20 missiles fly at once server-wide (`icbm.maxMissilesInFlight`), and all running blasts share one tick budget (`icbm.blastBudgetMs`).
- **Third-party content.** The jar ships models and textures adapted from HBM's Nuclear Tech Mod (LGPL-3.0), each folder with its license text, and a few ICBM algorithms ported from ICBM-Classic (MIT). BiblioCraft is an optional integration: nothing of it is shipped, and its pieces are placed only when it is installed. Chisel, Ztones and GregTech blocks are placed by name from the pack's own mods. See `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` and `docs/assets/CREDITS.md`.

## Known issues

- **Switching ICBM on in an existing world** leaves the silo complexes at the edge of explored land half generated. Use a new world, or explore new land, to see complete complexes.
- Silo complexes on distant planets (GalaxySpace and Amun-Ra worlds) have been generated and checked on a test server, not played.
- **T Ceti E** (an ocean world) has no complexes.
- **Spawners are slow to mine.** GregTech makes every mob spawner very hard, so a guard post takes a long time to break by hand. A warhead destroys it.
- Partly abandoned and abandoned complexes lose some spawners and furniture by design, so a quiet complex is not a bug.
- On Ross128, the hall sign names the complex's tier.
- Loading a launcher warhead is a crafting-grid recipe that NEI doesn't list; the launcher warhead's tooltip says how.
- The T3 conventional warheads share the T1 model at full size.
- Everything listed for [0.3.3-beta](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/releases/tag/0.3.3-beta) still applies.
