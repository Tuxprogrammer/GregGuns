# GregGuns 0.4.3-beta

> [!WARNING]
> **This is a beta.** This change was checked by unit tests, on a dev server with a client, and on the official GTNH 2.9.0 server pack, but has not had a long public playthrough. Back up your world before you update.

GregGuns Coins now go in the Vending Machine's coin slot, like the pack's own coins, and GregGuns trades take their price from your wallet.

## Read this first (server owners)

- **Tested on GTNH 2.9.0** (Vending Machine 0.4.103). Replace the jar on the server and on every client: both sides need it.
- **Drop-in from 0.4.2-beta.** No pack file or config changes. Coins, worlds and quest rewards carry over as they are.
- **Check the log.** At start the server logs "The GregGuns Coin is a Vending Machine coin kind: GregGuns trades are paid from the wallet". If it logs that the coin could not join the wallet instead, GregGuns trades take coins from the machine's input slots, as in 0.4.2-beta, and nothing else changes.

## Upgrading

### From 0.4.2-beta

Nothing to do beyond replacing the jar. GregGuns Coins you hold go into the coin slot from now on.

### From 0.4.1-beta or earlier

Read the [0.4.2-beta notes](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/releases/tag/0.4.2-beta) first; everything in them applies.

## Changed

- **GregGuns Coins go in the Vending Machine's coin slot.** The GregGuns Coin is now one of the machine's wallet coins, next to the pack's own. Put coins in the coin slot (or shift+right-click the machine holding them), and GregGuns trades take their price from your wallet, in any mix of coin values. Ejecting the balance gives GregGuns Coins back, largest first. The coin's tooltip says where it goes.
