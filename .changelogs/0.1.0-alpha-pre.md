# GregGuns 0.1.0-alpha (pre-release)

> [!WARNING]
> **This is an early alpha of a mod that is very much a work in progress.** Expect bugs, crashes, missing content, placeholder art and sound, and balance that is nowhere near final. **Back up your world before trying it**, and don't put it on a server or a save you care about.

GregGuns is a from-scratch, Techguns-inspired rewrite for **GT New Horizons** on Minecraft 1.7.10. It isn't a port and isn't affiliated with Techguns or its author. This first public build is meant for curious players and testers who want to see where it's heading.

## Read this first

- **Targets GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2.** It was tested in the Java 17+ (lwjgl3ify) instance only. Other pack versions and the Java 8 instance are untested.
- **Content is switched off by default.** After the first launch, edit `config/gregguns.cfg` and enable the modules, then restart:
  ```
  modules {
      B:guns=true
      B:armor=true
      B:machines=true
  }
  ```
- **Tested in single player only.** One person booted it in a GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2 client and fired the pistol, hand cannon and rifle. A dedicated server and multiplayer haven't been tested in the real pack yet.
- **Nothing is stable between alphas.** Items, recipes, machine IDs and config keys can change or disappear in later builds. That can delete items, or leave blocks broken, in worlds that used an older build.
- **Art and sound are placeholders or still in progress.** Several gun and armor models are being rebuilt. Some armor still uses interim models, and sounds are still being revised.
- **Balance hasn't started.** Damage, durability, recipe costs and tier gates are first drafts.

## What's in it

- **Guns:** 9mm Pistol, Revolver, Sawed Off, Hand Cannon, Bolt Action Rifle, with their ammo (stone, 9mm, shotgun shells, rifle bullets) and the 9mm magazine.
- **Melee and throwables:** Combat Knife, Crowbar, Stielgranate, Frag Grenade, Flashbang.
- **Armor:** tier 1 (bandit, miner, soldier), tier 2 (combat sets, commando, ranger veteran, beret), tier 3 (advanced combat, hazardous environment, power armor) and steam armor.
- **Gear for the extra slots:** night vision goggles, masks, oxygen gear, glider, jump pack, anti-gravity device and ammo pouch.
- **Machines:** the Ammo Press tiers, the gun-component chain into GregTech recipe maps, the Ammunition Works and Armory Gantry multiblocks, the Camo Bench, and the Reaction Chamber.

## Not in it yet

MV and higher guns, energy weapons, turrets and utility machines, NPCs and mobs, structures and worldgen, and the questbook. See [docs/ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/blob/master/docs/ROADMAP.md).

## Feedback

Bug reports are very welcome on [Issues](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/issues). Please include your GTNH version, Java version, the crash report or `fml-client-latest.log`, and what you were doing at the time.
