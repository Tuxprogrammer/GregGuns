# GregGuns 0.3.3-beta (pre-release)

> [!WARNING]
> **This is a beta.** Most of this build is new and has only been tested headless and by unit tests, not played. Back up your world first.

Polish for the pack: structures on Ross128, aim down sights, an in-game guide, pack fit from inside the jar, a balance pass and a Steam-age Steam armor.

## Headline

- **Structures on Ross128b and Ross128ba.** The ruins and bases now generate on both dimensions of the challenge start, with loot rolled per structure from MV up to UHV.
- **Aim down sights** for guns with iron sights or a red dot.
- **In-game guide.** GregGuns ships its own GuideNH guide.
- **Steam armor is a Steam-age set.** Crafted by hand from Bronze plates, burns Coal or Charcoal, with lower protection.
- **Better pack fit.** GregGuns adds itself to Backhand, AngerMod, Mobs Info and Thaumcraft lists in memory, and tidies its NEI entries.

## Upgrading

Drop-in from 0.3.2-beta. Replace the jar on the server and on every client. No config or world changes are needed, with one exception: **Steam Armor Plating no longer exists**, so any you hold disappears, and the Steam armor you already own keeps working with its new, lower stats.

## New

- **Structures on Ross128** (design record 0019). Config: `worldgen.structureRoss128` (default on) and `worldgen.ross128CeilingWeights`. Defenders and hole-spawner NPCs scale with each structure's rolled tier. The Aircraft Carrier and Submarine appear only where the dimension has ocean.
- **Aim down sights.** Right-click a gun with iron sights or a red dot (9mm Pistol, Golden Gun, Revolver, Golden Revolver, Thompson, Mac10, PDW, Combat Shotgun, Grenade Launcher, AK47, M4, Vector, LMG, Laser Rifle, Tesla Rifle, Scatter Laser Rifle). The gun slides to the screen center, the view narrows a little, the crosshair hides and sprinting stops. Guns without a scope get 25 percent tighter spread while aiming. Config: `aim.adsEnabled`, `aim.adsSpreadMultiplier`, `client.adsEnabled`, `client.adsFov`, `client.adsTransitionTicks`, `client.adsHideCrosshair`, `client.adsStopsSprint`.
- **In-game guide** (press G, or hold G over a GregGuns item): getting started, guns by tier, ammo, armor, machines and multiblocks, enemies and loot, pack notes. It loads the same without GuideNH.
- **NEI.** Item subsets `GregGuns.Guns`, `GregGuns.Ammo` and `GregGuns.Armor`; technical items are hidden.
- **Translations.** Every player-visible string is a lang key, including GT machine names and the questbook text. See `docs/translating.md`.

## Changed

- **Steam armor** (design record 0022). Hand-crafted from Bronze plates (3, 5, 4 and 2) and Heavy Cloth. Physical protection 14 (was 20); the powered perks are scaled down. It burns Coal or Charcoal (900 of 3,600 units each); a Compressed Air Tank still refills an empty chestplate. Repair uses Bronze plate. The Steam Armor quest moved to the Steam chapter.
- **Balance pass** (design record 0021). The LuV Alien Blaster holds 25 shots per charge (was 10). The IV Power Armor and Exo Suit chestplates hold 2,000,000 and 4,000,000 EU (were 720,000 and 1,440,000). Damage numbers are unchanged.
- **Pack fit.** New `compat.*` config keys (default on) control the in-memory list additions. The Attack Helicopter never rolls as an infernal mob. No other mod's config file is written.
- **Akimbo Pistols and Revolvers** are shown as LV on the player guides, matching the game.
- **Player guides** show Minecraft's real textures where they show vanilla items.

## Server owner notes

- Optional steps for packs that want them are in `docs/gregguns-spec/PACK-CHANGES.md`: each row says done in the jar, an optional server-owner step with the exact file, or not applicable.
- Structures on Ross128 follow `worldgen.structureDimensions` plus `worldgen.structureRoss128`. Turn the second off to keep them Overworld-only.
- The OpenModularTurrets overlap was investigated (design record 0020). Both turret mods can coexist; OMT turrets shoot GregGuns hostile NPCs, as intended.

## Known issues

- **Not played yet.** Aim down sights, the guide pages, the NEI changes and the pack-fit hooks (Backhand, AngerMod, Mobs Info, Thaumcraft) are covered by unit tests but have not been seen in a real client or pack. Each gun's sights may sit slightly off center; please report which.
- **Soak test.** A long server soak on the real pack was still running when this was cut; its findings will land in a later 0.3.x build.
- The Alien Bug Nest will not generate on Ross128ba without an admin change, because Galacticraft registers no biome types for that moon.
- Everything listed for [0.3.2-beta](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/releases/tag/0.3.2-beta) still applies.
