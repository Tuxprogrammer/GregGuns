# GregGuns 0.4.1-beta (pre-release)

> [!WARNING]
> **This is a beta.** The fixes in this build have been checked by unit tests and on test servers, but have not had a long public playthrough. Back up your world before you update.

Fixes from the first 0.4.0-beta playthrough: silo complexes that stay whole, escape hatches over water, a Vending Machine in every complex, structures that stand on level ground, every NPC with a place to spawn, drops from every NPC, and guns that reload one round at a time when they have no magazine.

## Read this first (server owners)

- **Targets GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2.** Replace the jar on the server and on every client.
- **Drop-in from 0.4.0-beta.** No pack file needs changing. An existing `config/gregguns.cfg` keeps the values it was written with and gains the new keys listed under "Config".

## Upgrading

### From 0.4.0-beta

- **Existing structures and silo complexes keep what they were built with.** The foundations, the whole-complex keep, the escape riser and the Vending Machine appear in structures and complexes generated after the update. `/gregguns locate rebuild <cellX> <cellZ>` regenerates one silo complex in place.
- **Existing configs** keep their values; set the new keys yourself if you want to change them.

### From 0.4.0-alpha or earlier

Read the [0.4.0-beta notes](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/releases/tag/0.4.0-beta) first; everything in them applies.

## New

### Silo complexes

- **A Vending Machine in every complex.** One per complex, against a wall of a dining hall (a Mess Galley or Cafeteria), or in the Lounge when the complex has no dining hall. In a Bravo or Charlie complex it works, with the GregGuns Coin trades. In an Alpha ruin its controller stands dark with the casing knocked out; put a casing back (iron plates and foil) and it forms.
- **Complexes stay whole after world generation.** GregTech ore veins and stone layers (however tall, Fortunate One included), lava lakes, dungeons and other mods' generation leave no ore, stone or holes in rooms, halls, shafts or the escape ladder; the complex puts back whatever they changed, once. Ore inside the complex is lost; the rock between rooms keeps its ore.
- **Escape hatches, silo shafts and radar huts over water.** Under a river or lake, the escape ladder climbs inside a concrete riser to a trapdoor one block above the water, a silo shaft rises through the water to its mouth, and a radar hut stands on a foundation from the bed. On land the hatch stays a camouflaged trapdoor in the ground.

### NPCs

- **Every NPC has a home.** Zombie Pigman Soldiers and Super Mutants spawn in the Nether, the mutants tougher the deeper in you go, with a rare Cyberdemon far out and alone; Commando squads of two or three roam the End's outer islands, away from the dragon's island; one Military Base in five is a red base, where Dictator Dave holds his post by the red flag; and the Outcast guards silo complexes of LuV and up.
- **Every NPC drops something.** A quarter to three quarters of a magazine of loose rounds for its gun (a Thompson gives 5 to 15 9mm bullets), a full tank, or parts of an energy gun. Zombie Farmers and Miners, Skeleton Soldiers, Zombie Pigman Soldiers and Super Mutants also drop ammo for their guns, Stormtroopers drop energy-weapon parts, and the Outcast has a full loot table. A player kill can drop a copy of the gun (2% plus 1% per Looting level, never from bosses). Mobs Info lists the drops.
- **Combat is gentler by default, and every part of it is a config key** (design record 0027). Gun and projectile hits no longer knock their target back (`combat.knockback`); explosions still throw. NPC gun damage follows the gun's tier: full up to MV, then 0.85 at HV, 0.7 at EV, 0.55 at IV, 0.45 at LuV and 0.4 at ZPM; Elite, Ultra, Infernal and boss NPCs shoot at 0.75 on top, and NPC guns get half their armor penetration against players. NPCs aim true within 5 blocks and lose accuracy beyond that (about half the shots hit at 20 blocks, one in ten at 30). Player guns and the Rampart turret are unchanged. `docs/config.md`, "Make combat harder", lists the values for a harder game.
- The EV T3 Combat set and the IV Exo Suit protect 23 physical (92%), and 19 against energy, fire, ice, lightning and explosions.

### Guns

- **Reloading.** A magazine gun with no loaded magazine at hand loads one loose round per reload (two on the Akimbo Mac10s). The Revolvers, Golden Revolvers, Combat Shotgun and Sawed Off load one chamber at a time, a trigger pull stops the reload and fires what is loaded, and held without firing for 2 seconds they top themselves up (`guns.idleReloadDelayTicks`, 0 turns it off). Hold Shift over a gun for its reload rule.
- **Bandages and the Medkit** heal while you hold right-click: Bandages (LV) take 3 seconds and heal 1 heart at once, then Regeneration I for 15 seconds; the Medkit (MV) takes 2.5 seconds, heals 3 hearts and gives Regeneration II for 20 seconds. Your arm swings and an eating sound plays while you hold, with a burp at the end. At full health, a right-click says there is nothing to heal. <!-- PEER (items, D361): facts from the items agent's message; re-check against beta/041-items after it merges. -->

## Changed

- **Structures carve their own site and bring their own foundation.** Every surface structure, the Military Base with all its tents, buildings, roads, fence and flag pad included, stands on solid ground filled down to the natural ground, with no hillside, tree or overhang left inside or over it, and a cut bank around it on slopes. The Big Bunker also gets a foundation under its buried floor. The Aircraft Carrier, the Submarine and the Alien Bug Nest keep their own footing.
- **Complexes avoid oceans and steep slopes.** A complex is never placed in an ocean or on a steep slope; rivers and lakes count as land.
- GregGuns single blocks, hatches and items read "Added by: GregGuns" in their tooltips.
- Super Mutants stand on the ground; their legs no longer sink into the floor.
- **Guides, quests and sheets.** The in-game guide, Mobs Info text, quest text and the player infographics (Enemies and Loot, Abandoned Silos, Getting Started, Ammo and Magazines) describe all of the above, and name the real default keys: **R** reload, **M** night vision, **]** ammo pouch and **;** glider or anti-gravity device.

## Config

Every key is documented in `docs/config.md`.

- **New keys:** `guns.idleReloadDelayTicks`, the Nether and End spawn tables (`npcs.netherSpawnDimensions`, `npcs.endSpawnDimensions`, `npcs.weightNether*`, `npcs.weightEndCommando`, `npcs.endMinDistance`, `npcs.bossSpacing`), `npcs.outcast` and `worldgen.redBaseChanceMilitaryBase`.
- **New `combat` category:** `combat.knockback` (false), `combat.knockbackMultiplier` (1.0), `combat.npcDamageSteam`, `combat.npcDamageLV`, `combat.npcDamageMV` (1.0), `combat.npcDamageHV` (0.85), `combat.npcDamageEV` (0.7), `combat.npcDamageIV` (0.55), `combat.npcDamageLuV` (0.45), `combat.npcDamageZPM` (0.4), `combat.npcEliteDamage` (0.75), `combat.npcAccuracyRange` (5.0), `combat.npcSpreadPerBlock` (0.12) and `combat.npcMaxSpread` (4.0).
- **Changed default:** `damagefactors.armorPenetrationMultiplierNPCtoPlayer` 1.0 to 0.5.

## Known issues

- Silo complexes on distant planets (GalaxySpace and Amun-Ra worlds) have been generated and checked on a test server, not played.
- **T Ceti E** (an ocean world) has no complexes.
- **Spawners are slow to mine.** GregTech makes every mob spawner very hard, so a guard spawner takes a long time to break by hand. A warhead destroys it.
- **Blast doors stop a walk, not a pickaxe.** Their slats are thin and break quickly. A GT cable or lever placed beside a door can open or shut it, as with any Carpenter's door.
- The T3 conventional warheads share the T1 model at full size.
- Everything listed for [0.3.3-beta](https://github.com/Tuxprogrammer/GregGuns/releases/tag/0.3.3-beta) still applies.
