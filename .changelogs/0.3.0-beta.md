# GregGuns 0.3.0-beta (pre-release)

> [!WARNING]
> **This is a beta, not a finished mod.** It is the first feature-complete build, but balance is a first draft and the in-game client pass is still in progress (see Known issues). Back up your world before you try it.

GregGuns is a from-scratch, Techguns-inspired rewrite for **GT New Horizons** on Minecraft 1.7.10. It isn't a port and isn't affiliated with Techguns or its author.

## Headline

0.3.0 is the first **feature-complete** build: every roadmap milestone from M0 to M8 is in. That means the full ballistic and energy arsenal, armor and accessories, the production chain and GregTech machines, utility and defense machines, NPCs with natural spawning, and worldgen structures. It replaces the alpha pre-releases. What remains is pack integration, the questbook and a balance pass (M9).

- **Targets GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2.**
- The automated suites are green (unit tests and the headless server scenarios). The batched in-game client pass is not finished, so expect visual rough edges.

## Upgrading from 0.2.0-alpha

### Breaking or important

- **All modules now default to on.** The 0.2.0 jar shipped with every module off, and the config file keeps whatever value it was written with. If you enabled nothing, or only some modules, your existing `config/gregguns.cfg` may still say `false`. Open it and check the `modules` block (`guns`, `armor`, `machines`, `energyWeapons`, `utility`, `npcs`, `worldgen`). Delete the file to get the new defaults. Module flags need a restart and must match on server and clients.
- **`npcs` and `worldgen` are on by default.** Zombies and soldiers will now spawn naturally in the Overworld and structures will generate in new chunks. Turn them off in the `modules` block if you don't want that. Existing chunks are not touched.
- **The Reaction Chamber and the Retort are removed** (design record 0012). Placed ones become inert "retired" blocks you can break, and anything inside them, including a run in progress, is lost. Their item forms show a "retired" tooltip and do nothing. Their machine IDs (20106 to 20108) are reserved forever. Retort casings remain as decorative blocks. The `machines.*` config keys for them are gone.
- **The Ammunition Works and the Armory Gantry now need an interior lamp.** The structure has a required lamp position: glowstone, a redstone lamp, or any block with light level 12 or more. Multiblocks built before this version unform until you add one; the NEI structure preview and the controller tooltip show where it goes.
- **Heat Ray Focus is gone.** Stacks in inventories and chests vanish with no refund.
- **The bio-ammo chain changed.** The Bio Tank now holds **E. coli culture fluid** (from BartWorks), not raw Biomass. The path is: Brewery makes Biomass, the Bio Lab makes the E. coli dish, the Bacterial Vat turns Biomass into culture fluid, and a Fluid Canner fills the Bio Tank. To keep accepting plain Biomass, set `bioGun.acceptBiomass=true`.

### Recipe changes

- **Laser Focus** is now made in the GT **Laser Engraver** (EV), and the **Anti Gravity Core** in the GT **Assembler** (IV). The Emitter is consumed per Laser Focus, which makes it a little steeper than before.
- The **Fuel, Bio and Compressed Air Tanks** are built from GT Large Fluid Cells (stainless, titanium and aluminum).
- **Cybernetic Parts** use the BartWorks **Plasma Membrane**.
- **Circuit changes** from the recipe-clash fixes: Blaster Rifle, HEV Boots, the turret plates, Carbon Bastion and a few ammo and magazine rows moved to different circuits, and the Laser Focus now takes a Ruby, so none of them shadows an existing GTNH recipe. Check any custom scripts or quests that name those inputs.
- **Ammo takes 4 times longer to make.** The duration multiplier is one constant, applied to every ammo press recipe.
- **The Steam Ammo Press runs LV recipes** as well.
- Armor and accessories now have GT recycling data, so GT's recycling machines return their materials.

### Item order changed

Armor, utility items and NPC spawn eggs are now registered in tier and build order, so creative tabs and NEI list them differently. Existing items are safe: Forge remaps by name, not by position.

## New since 0.2.0

**NPCs and spawning**
- All 19 mobs are in: zombie farmer, miner, soldier and pigman soldier; skeleton soldier; army soldier; commando; bandit; outcast; Stormtrooper; Psycho Steve; Dictator Dave; three super mutant grades; alien bug; Cyberdemon; and the attack helicopter. Each has a spawn egg.
- Natural spawning is gated by faction, weight, dimension and distance from world spawn, with per-chunk and per-player caps.
- Elite, ultra and infernal NPCs work with **Infernal Mobs** and add tiered GregGuns drops.
- Camp Flags spawn defenders and send a helicopter when broken.

**Worldgen**
- Structures: Small Factory, Train Station, Gas Station, Medium House, Big Bunker, Military Base, Aircraft Carrier, Submarine and the Alien Bug Nest (off by default).
- Tiered loot chests that fill when first opened, matched to where the structure spawns.
- Zombie Hole and Bug Hole spawner blocks.
- Placement never triggers cascading chunk generation (checked headless).

**Weapons and effects**
- Energy and exotic weapons now have fixed beam origins: first-person beams start at the muzzle.
- All 43 gun icons were regenerated from the models.
- Tooltips are tier-colored and show each gun's magazine capacity.

**Machines**
- Multiblock interiors for the Ammunition Works and the Rampart, and Ammunition Works glass.
- The Armory Gantry multiblock.
- The Steam Ammo Press has its own GUI.
- Every GregGuns machine shows **"Added by: GregGuns"**, so an NEI search for it lists them all.

**Utility**
- The Explosive Charge GUIs were rebuilt with ModularUI2, and only the owner can use the controls.
- Turret and Camp Flag behavior is tunable in `utility.*`.

**Armor**
- Armor and accessory icons match their worn looks.
- The Stormtrooper wears the HEV camo 1 kit.

## Fixes

- **Shaders and water:** leaked OpenGL state in the beam, particle, ammo press, gun, mob and GUI render paths is now restored after each draw. This is what could break water or shader packs. Verification with shaders is still pending (see Known issues).
- **Beam origin:** first-person beams start at the muzzle.
- **Minigun:** the rotor spins about the barrel axis.
- **Retort crash:** machines now register on both sides, so a module mismatch no longer crashes rendering (a client NPE on the Retort item).
- **Charge render:** Explosive Charge items render like vanilla block items, with no wrong look or left offset, and are larger in the first-person grip.
- **Weapons chest:** the lid no longer z-fights the body.
- **Ammo press output stall:** presses now fill every output slot, so they no longer stop with room left.
- **GUI open:** the Repair Bench, Camo Bench and Turret GUIs open when you right-click them.
- Recipe clashes with GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2 were fixed (see Server owner notes).

## Server owner notes

**Config keys worth knowing** (full list in `docs/config.md`):
- `modules.*`: on/off per area. `worldgen` needs `npcs`.
- NPCs: `npcs.spawnDimensions` (default Overworld only), `npcs.perChunkCap` (4), `npcs.perPlayerCap` (8), the per-faction switches, the `npcs.weight*` spawn weights and `npcs.distanceLevel0/1/2` for how danger grows with distance. A weight of 0 turns a mob off. `npcs.alienBug`, `cyberdemon` and `helicopter` are off.
- Worldgen: `worldgen.spawnStructures`, `worldgen.enabled<Id>` per structure, `worldgen.structureDimensions`, `worldgen.structureWorldTypes` (flat worlds are excluded), `worldgen.spacing*` and `worldgen.biomeBlacklist<Id>`. The Alien Bug Nest is off behind `worldgen.enabledAlienBugNest`.
- **`explosions.blockDamage`** (default **false**): whether GregGuns explosions remove blocks. Off, they only hurt entities. It also gates the Bio Gun blob. Block removal goes through the usual protection hooks, and NPC blasts also need the `mobGriefing` gamerule.
- `bioGun.acceptBiomass` (default false): lets the Bio Tank take plain GT Biomass.
- `damagefactors.*` for PvP and turret damage. Set `allow-flight=true` in `server.properties` if players use flight gear.

**Performance.** A dedicated-server soak test ran 100 mixed NPCs fighting for 2,400 ticks: mean tick time 2.78 ms against a 5.99 ms baseline, with all NPCs alive throughout. That machine is faster than a typical player server, so treat the numbers as a sanity check, not a promise. The multiplayer soak on the real pack is part of M9. Details: `docs/gregguns-spec/10-entities-npcs/m7-exit-gate.md`.

**Pack compatibility.** Built and tested against GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2. A mechanical recipe-clash review (a dump of about 248,000 recipes from a full server, diffed against ours) is clean: no exact or shadowing clashes. See `docs/testing/recipe-clash-beta.md`.
- Pack scripts must not remove the Bacterial Vat and Bio Lab maps, the HV Centrifuge Plasma Membrane recipe or the Large Fluid Cell Assembler recipes, or the related GregGuns recipes become uncraftable. `/gregguns dumprecipes` (needs `debug.recipeDump`) is the check.
- The pack-side changes (Infernal Mobs, Mobs Info and similar configs, the questbook) are not applied yet. They are listed in `docs/gregguns-spec/PACK-CHANGES.md` and land with M9.
- GregGuns uses MTE IDs 20100 to 20115. 20106 to 20108 are retired and must not be reused.
